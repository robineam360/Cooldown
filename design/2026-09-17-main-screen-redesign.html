CCRM-72 (Main Screen Redesign) & CCRM-73 (Model Cap Chart) · 2026-09-17 · rev C

The main screen: compact cards, a layout you can rearrange, one status line — and a chart for the Fable cap

CCRM-72 (Main Screen Redesign) is three changes to ProfileScreen: a global Comfortable/Compact density chip (Compact folds each card to a title, bar and one line, tap to expand the chart in place); per-account card visibility and order via a new layout sheet (CCRM-25 (Card Layout) + CCRM-35 (Layout Reset)); and one status line replacing the Refresh button plus the "Last success/Last attempt" pair. CCRM-73 (Model Cap Chart) rides in the same 7-day card: a toggle between the pool ("All") and any per-model cap (Fable today) so the chart, pace readout and estimate follow whichever series is selected — the data half already landed (forward-only history tags, 421 tests green); this wireframe draws the UI half.

Rev C — for review before any code is written — working agreement 2 (wireframe first). Not yet approved. Rev C is Robin's rev B review, applied: "All models" renamed to "All" everywhere (§4/§5/§11); the inner-screen 7-day card redrawn as two chart columns when the account has a cap — the one place Comfortable deliberately differs from v1.6 (§8/§11); and the Free plan question decided — stays bare (§7/§11). No open questions remain.

2 · The status line — anatomy

One row, directly under the last visible card — the last one shown above "More", if any are folded behind it (§9); the invariant there guarantees at least one always is. Reuses AccountStatusLine's wording, not a new grammar: Checked <ago> from the last success, a spinner in place of the ↻ glyph while a refresh is in flight, and a second clause — · Tried <ago> — only when the last attempt failed and happened after the last success (so a healthy account never shows two timestamps for one fact). ErrorNotice renders exactly as it does today, directly beneath.

8 · Inner screen ~750dp

Above the two-pane width the Main screen still shows one account per page (unchanged from today — this is not the History screen's two-column split). The 5-hour card only ever has one series, so it just gets a taller chart: ContentColumn(maxWidth = ChartColumnMaxWidth) caps the column at 760dp, so on a 750dp inner display the card content is ~678dp wide and chartHeight(678dp) lands at 237dp (678 × 0.35, inside the 180–300 clamp) rather than the 180dp floor a phone hits.

The 7-day card runs two chart columns when the account has a cap — the width the 5-hour card just spends on a taller chart, the 7-day card spends on a second one, since it already has a second series to show. A 16dp gutter splits the ~678dp card content into two ~331dp columns, each a full stack: SubBar row, bar, chart, pace line, estimate line, reset row. Left column is All; right is the cap. A column this narrow floors its own chart back at 180dp — chartHeight(331dp) = 331 × 0.35 ≈ 116, clamped to 180 — the same floor a phone hits, so nothing here is stretched thin to fill the width. Two caps keep it at two columns, not three: the right column carries a small chip pair (Sonnet | Opus) above its chart, and the selected cap's full stack follows it — no toggle chips at all in the one-cap case, since both columns are already visible side by side. No caps (ChatGPT, Free) keeps the single full-width column at 237dp, exactly as today. This applies in Comfortable and in Compact-expanded; Compact-collapsed on the inner screen stays the same stacked SubBars + one line it is on the phone (§4) — width doesn't change a collapsed card's shape, only an open one's.

This is the one place Comfortable deliberately differs from v1.6, at Robin's request, 2026-09-17 — every other Comfortable card is pixel-identical to today's (§1). A single 331dp-floored column reading half as wide as the 5-hour card above it, with a second series and nothing drawn for it, was the case that didn't survive review.

9 · The layout sheet (CCRM-25 (Card Layout))

Entry point: a new ⋮ in the Main top bar, after the history and settings icons, opening "Main screen layout" — drawn here as the bar really is today: the two provider marks and "Cooldown" on the left (no back chevron; the Main screen is the root destination, per MainActivity's navigationIcon, which only appears on Settings/History), the history (calendar) and settings icons on the right. The ⋮ itself is conditional: it shows only when the selected account has two or more cards to arrange. A one-card account (a plan with neither a 5-hour window nor credits, say) has nothing the sheet could hide or fold under the never-blank invariant — opening it would offer a single row with its switch already disabled, which is not a sheet worth having an entry point for. The sheet is the CCRM-71 (Account Order) idiom — ReorderAccountsSheet/ReorderRow's 56dp rows and 48dp drag-handle target, reused rather than redrawn — one row per card (5-hour window, 7-day window, Usage credits), each with a show/hide switch and a drag handle; a dashed "Behind More" divider a card can be dragged under; a "Reset layout" text button in the RemoveAccountDialog shape.

Decisions this wireframe takes

  1. Comfortable is a strict no-op on the cards. Only the footer changes (status line replaces Refresh + two timestamps) — §1 proves it side by side rather than asserting it.
  2. The chevron lives in the title row, in both densities, not on the collapsed-only reset/pace line — that line disappears entirely when expanded, so the chevron needed a home that survives both states.
  3. The 96–110dp collapsed-card target is a single-bar number. The 7-day card is explicitly exempted by the roadmap's own next sentence ("keeps its SubBars in both densities") — §4/§11's table gives its real heights (143/196/249dp) rather than forcing SubBars to fit a budget sized for one bar.
  4. The whole card is the tap target for expand/collapse — no separate disclosure icon button, so the hit area is the full 107dp row rather than a 24dp glyph.
  5. Free plan draws today's bare behaviour (nothing above the status line, per CCBG-27 (Free Plan 403)) rather than inventing new copy the roadmap didn't ask for — decided on review, 2026-09-17 (below).
  6. The invariant surfaces as a disabled switch + static helper text, not a toast on a blocked tap — visible before the user tries the thing that would break it.
  7. Reset layout uses RemoveAccountDialog's shape but not its error-red confirm button — resetting layout is reversible (drag it back) where removing an account destroys a year of history; the confirm button stays the accent colour.
  8. Usage credits is one shape in both densities — it never had a chart, so Compact has nothing to fold: no chevron, no collapsed/expanded distinction, header + bar + "$380.28 left · covers you when you hit your plan limits" always (§6/§7).
  9. The 7-day chart toggle is per account and persisted (UsageCache.weeklyChart), not per card-open-state — collapsing or expanding the 7-day card never changes which series (All vs a cap) is selected; only tapping a chip does.
  10. The "Behind More" divider needs no explainer line — the sheet's own subtitle, "Show, hide and reorder the cards on this account's screen," already says what dragging a row past it does; a second line would repeat it.
  11. No inner fold for a third+ SubBar (the 249dp two-cap collapsed height stands). Caps are real data, not decoration to hide, and two caps in one account only happens on older payloads (CCRM-73 (Model Cap Chart)'s own UI-half note: "Fable; Sonnet + Opus on older payloads") — folding a rare case inside an already-collapsed card would add a control for something that almost never occurs.
  12. The top-bar ⋮ only shows once an account has two or more cards to arrange (§2/§9) — a single-card account can't hide or fold anything under the never-blank invariant, so the sheet behind it would be a single disabled row.

Decided on review, 2026-09-17

  1. Free plan (§7c) stays bare, as today — no Accounts-screen-style notice added under the status line.
  2. "All", not "All models" — the 7-day card's top SubBar row and the CCRM-73 (Model Cap Chart) toggle chip both read "All" everywhere in the wireframe (§4, §5, §8).
  3. The inner-screen 7-day card runs two chart columns when the account has a cap: All on the left, the cap on the right, each with its own full stack (SubBar, bar, chart, pace, estimate, reset row). Two caps put a small chip pair (e.g. Sonnet | Opus) above the right column's chart; no caps keeps one full-width column, exactly as today (§8). This is the one place Comfortable deliberately differs from v1.6.
