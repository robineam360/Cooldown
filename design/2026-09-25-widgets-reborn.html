CCRM-78 (Widgets Reborn) · 2026-09-25 · rev A

Four home-screen faces, none of them a clone of the app

A status-bar-style Ring (CCRM-79 (Ring Face)) at two sizes; a Number face (CCRM-80 (Number Face)) carrying the always-on notification's Huge-number geometry through three widths, gaining an absolute reset line and then a window/account control row as it grows; a new Countdown face (CCRM-81 (Countdown Face)) that answers "when does it come back" as a live chronometer or an absolute clock time, never a static relative one; and a new Accounts Strip (CCRM-82 (Accounts Strip)) — every account as its own small ring, never summed. Riding along: the measured Duet label clamp (CCBG-24 (Duet Label Clamp)), the Accounts-tab "Clear usage history…" action (CCRM-14 (Clear History)), the Debug-section synthetic-series override (CCRM-15 (Above-Pace Verification)), the Faces gallery (CCRM-84 (Faces Gallery)) and the Share Card (CCRM-24 (Share Card)).

Rev A — for review. Nothing is approved yet.

Rules on every tile
  • R1 wording — "5h" and "Weekly", never "5-hour window" or "7-day".
  • R2 clocks — every time is 12-hour, always; a day is added only past 24h out ("Sat 9:10 PM"). No 24-hour time appears anywhere on this page.
  • R4 time is a claim — every time is live (a ticking chronometer, captioned) or absolute ("Resets 9:10 PM", "at 9:10 PM"). Never a static relative time — no countdown-language remaining-time phrasing, no elapsed-since-last-refresh caption, no refresh glyph.
  • R6 stale — the figure dims to 0.5, the ring/bar to 0.45, with "Stale" where a sub-line exists.
  • No combined figure anywhere — least of all on the Strip.

S1 (normal), dark and light Solid (the account's Room card colour, radius = the system system_app_widget_background_radius, drawn at 24 dp), and Transparent over a light and a dark wallpaper — including the mismatched case, drawn honestly.
