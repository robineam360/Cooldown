CCBG-34 (Account Display Name) · wireframe

Which account is which

Awaiting Robin's approval

Raja's feedback, read together with Robin's 2026-09-25 call: once an account isn't named by hand, there's no way to tell the eam360.com login from the gmail.com one apart from opening each and checking. The fix is to show the email the account signed in with. This sheet is a revision of design/accounts-redesign-wireframe.html (CCRM-65 (Accounts Redesign)) — same tokens, same AccountCard markup, one new line and one new dialog row.

1. Placement

Two additions inside the app, plus a rename-time suggestion. Reuses the header row built for CCBG-42 (Kebab Drift) — mark, label, dot, chip, ⋮ — and the ⋮ → Details dialog built for CCRM-65 (Accounts Redesign).

  • Accounts card: a small line under the account name, inside the header block, above "Checked 1m ago". bodySmall, onSurfaceVariant, single line, ellipsized. Starts where the name starts — after the 20dp mark and the 6dp gap, so it reads as a subtitle of the label, not a new column.
  • ⋮ → Details: an "Email" row in AccountDetailsDialog (app/src/main/java/com/robin/claudeusage/SettingsScreen.kt:1241).
  • Rename dialog: when an account is added or renamed, the field offers a suggestion drawn from the email — see §6.
Never on the lock-screen notification, the widgets, or the share card — all visible to anyone holding the phone — and it stays out of the diagnostics log, whose rule is "no emails." Only the in-app Settings → Accounts card and its Details/Rename dialogs show it.

6. Questions for Robin

Q1 — Ellipsis direction for a long email. Middle (keep the start of the local part and the whole @domain) or end (today's default TextOverflow.Ellipsis)?
Recommend middle — the domain is the whole point of CCBG-34 (Account Display Name); an end-ellipsis on a long local part (e.g. "robin.richard.rajan.longname…") would hide exactly the thing this line exists to show. Implementation is two Texts in a Row, split at "@" — no experimental API needed.

Decision:

Q2 — Unknown email (state 2): nothing, or a placeholder?
Recommend nothing — a placeholder ("email pending…") is one more line that changes shape the moment the next daily profile fetch lands, for a case that's already rare (only an account signed in before this shipped, until its next fetch).

Decision:

Q3 — Label already equals the email (state 5): show the line anyway, or suppress it?
Recommend suppress — repeating the exact same text on the line below adds no information and just spends a line.

Decision:

Q4 — Rename suggestion: local part or full email, as a chip or as supporting text?
Recommend the local part, as a chip — ProfileRegistry.MAX_LABEL is 16 characters (app/src/main/java/com/robin/claudeusage/data/ProfileRegistry.kt:155), so a full email suggestion would be truncated ugly for most real addresses (even "robin@eam360.com" is 17). The field's supportingText slot is already the character counter, so the suggestion needs its own spot — a small tappable chip reads clearly there without crowding the counter.
