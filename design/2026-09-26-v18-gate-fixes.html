The Step 7 re-run on the Fold 7 (captures in design/research/2026-09-26-v18-device-pass/) left three visible defects. Robin chose to fix all three before the release. Each section shows today and the proposal. Nothing is built until this page is approved.

What does not change
  • The single-account notification (Second = None) keeps notif_big_number.xml byte for byte.
  • The Huge-number figure stays 30 sp bold in the Duet. Only where the label sits moves.
  • Every widget face and frame other than the cover-height Strip 4×1 is untouched. Rev D's own 84 dp-tall frames draw exactly as today.

1 · CCBG-39 (Inner Duet Squeeze) — the collapsed Duet keeps one letter of each name

A notification is one layout for every width: the same row shows on the cover, on the unfolded shade and on a regular phone, and RemoteViews cannot measure where it is. So the fix has to work at 101 dp halves and at 156 dp with the same views. Today the label shares the half's width with the 30 sp figure, so a narrow half leaves it ~20 dp ("P…"). Rev G gives the label its own line across the whole half, and the bar and figure share the line under it.

2 · Strip 4×1 type on One UI's cover (Fable's open note)

One UI draws cover widgets at 71 %, so rev F's 9.5 sp label reads as 6.8 sp, smaller than any system text. The cover's 4×1 frame is 108 dp tall, which leaves ~13 dp unused under the name. Rev G, on 4×1 frames at least 100 dp tall only: ring ceiling Ø56 → Ø64, bore figure 13 → 16 sp, "Weekly" tag 8 → 9 sp, "Free" 13 → 15 sp, label 9.5 → 12 sp (reads 8.6 sp). Four cells still fit: 72 dp each at 333 dp, and "Product" at 12 sp with its mark is ~59 dp. Under 100 dp, rev D's numbers stand.

3 · CCBG-40 (Picker Preview Ellipsis) — the Countdown preview loses its reset time

One UI lays the picker preview out at about 110 × 76 dp, not at a placed frame. "2:41:07" at 24 sp leaves no room beside it for "at 9:10 PM": the cover drops it silently and the inner shows "at…". A stacked line needs ~91 dp of height, so it does not fit either. The preview is the only thing that changes; placed Countdowns keep their layouts.
