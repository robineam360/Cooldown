CCBG-44 (Widget Fill) · wireframe rev H

Widgets that fill their frame

Fable's review (research/2026-09-26-ccbg44-fable-review.md) found every widget face drawing a rev-D/F-sized block inside a much bigger reported frame and leaving the rest as air — a 4×3 Ring with 42 visible dp of nothing above and below its ring, a Number whose 6×2 leaves two ~35 dp bands, a Countdown that never grows past its 2×1 content, a Strip whose rings sit 70 visible dp apart. Rev H is the fill rule: one hero per face (ring Ø, figure sp, count sp) sized to the room the frame's fixed secondary rows leave, up to a cap, with spare splitting evenly above and below rather than sitting between rows. Every number on this page is drawn from Fable's formulas, to scale, at 1 dp = 1 px reported.

0 · The problem, in Robin's own units

Four shots from the Fold 7 cover, reported dp as dumpsys appwidget gave them (multiply by 0.714 for what the eye sees). Fable's one-line diagnosis under each.

The fill rule (spec §2)
  • Each face has one hero (ring Ø, figure sp, count sp) and fixed secondary rows of known height. Rows are added in a fixed order as room allows, dropped in reverse at the narrow end. The hero takes the height (or width) the rows leave, up to a cap. Spare never sits between rows — what remains after the cap splits evenly above and below the block. Left-aligned as rev D; rings centred. Pads unchanged (8/12/14).
  • Tiers (by the reported frame, bucketFor unchanged): S H<140 — narrow W<240 / wide ≥240; T H≥140 and W/H<1.35; W H≥140 and W/H≥1.35.
  • Roomy type at inner H ≥ 200: label 15, sub 13, caption 12, marks 14, stamp 10, gaps 8 — else rev D's 13/12/11/12–14/10, gaps 6.
  • Caps: Ring Ø160 (companion Ø100), Strip Ø120; Number figure 72 sp (96 at inner H ≥ 300), count 80 sp.

Six-column cover frame table this page draws to (reported dp): 1 col 84, 2 cols 156, 3 cols 244, 4 cols 333, 6 cols 510; rows 108 / 237 / 366. Inner screen: 2×2 211×291, 2×1 211×126, 4×1 467×126, 4×2 467×291 (derived — see §7 Open items).

1 · Ring (CCRM-79)

Reserve under the ring: T tier 56 dp (name 18 + reset 16 + stamp 14 + gaps 8); W tier's column runs beside the ring instead of under it. Drop order at the narrow end: companion → stamp → reset → name.

2 · Number (CCRM-80)

Label+figure share one row from 3×1 up (S narrow stacks them); bar under; sub row under the bar; controls (5h|Weekly chips + account cycler) appear only once the frame has a dedicated bottom row for them (2×2 T narrow, and every T/W large frame).

3 · Countdown (CCRM-81)

A left-aligned column from 2×2 up: caption, the live count, an absolute “at” time, a figure row (percent LEFT + when it runs out), a bar, then label and stamp pinned to the bottom. 4×3 is not a placement this face is for — see the note at the end of this section.

A 4×3 Countdown was measured (count 66sp, 149 dp of spare below the figure row) and rejected — no placement offers it that shape, so it is not drawn as a mock here. A two-column layout at W tier was also measured and rejected (it forces the count under 40 sp and reopens the bands rev H exists to close).

4 · All accounts (Strip, CCRM-82)

Every account is its own ring, never summed. S tier (one row): a name line under each ring, pitch capped at Ø+56 so the row never over-spreads; the reset joins the name inline once the account's own slot is ≥ Ø+70 wide. T/W tier (two rows): Ø from the row's own height, name+reset appear only once Ø ≥ 100, the “Weekly” tag once Ø ≥ 110.

5 · States

One tight and one roomy frame per face, at five states: 100% (red, the ring's × and the bar's/count's red fill), above pace (both the pace-exceeded slice and a ladder colour — pct 94, elapsed 85, so both cues show together), stale (dimmed 0.5, R6), no reading yet (“—”), and unassigned (spec §3 Q4 wording — see captions).

6 · Rules that change

  1. R10 budget 2 → 4 MB. The host cap is ~6 B × display px (≈16 MB on a 1080×2520 phone), and bitmaps over 16 KB cross Binder as ashmem blobs, so the 1 MB transaction limit was never the real bound. “At most three buckets” and “five bitmaps per bucket” stay unchanged — tiers live inside buckets. revF_revDFramesKeepRevDGeometry becomes a rev H invariance test (the 1×1 Ø64 cap and the Countdown gaps change on rev D's own frames).
  2. CCRM-79 (Ring Face) “Omits: reset, weekly” is reversed: the Ring gains an absolute reset line from 2×2 up, and a companion Weekly ring on 4×3/6×2. Transitions counts both windows for a Ring whose account has both.
  3. CCRM-80 (Number Face) — controls land on the new 2×2 Number (Q3); WidgetHost.wire wires by tier, not bucket.
  4. Rev F d.4 (“extra height stays empty”) is reversed — that is this bug. The “as of” stamp gains two homes it didn't have before: the Ring's S-wide column and the Number's 2×2.

Literal reading, not a measured value.
  • Number 2×2's figure (52 sp) is Fable's literal number; recomputing min(innerW/2.4, 72) from this page's own padFor gives 55, not 52 — used the spec's stated value rather than my recomputation.
  • Strip's S-tier ring size (Ø56) has no formula in Fable's table (only the T/W row gives one) — used one of rev D's own historical ceilings, cited in the spec's intro. The 6×1 and inner 4×1 “inline reset” demo also reads “cell width” as the nominal per-account share of the row (before the pitch cap centres the group), since a literal reading where cell width equals the capped pitch can never clear the Ø+70 threshold (a cap of Ø+56 is always short of Ø+70 by construction) — worth Fable's or Robin's check.
  • Companion ring's Weekly percentage (52%, elapsed 40) for Work is invented — the task's fixture data gives only a 5h reading per account, no Weekly figure.
  • Number 4×3's figure (88 sp) is this page's own computation from the formula with the stated cap raised to 96; Fable's text gives the cap but not the resulting number.
  • "Above pace / orange ladder" state (§5) is drawn as pct 94 / elapsed 85 so the pace-exceeded slice and the severity ladder's orange band show at once; the spec names both cues but doesn't give a shared fixture.
  • Which frames stand in for "one tight and one roomy" per face (§5) is this page's own choice (1×1/4×2 for the Ring, 2×1/4×2 for the rest) — the spec's Q4 wording happens to name exactly those tight frames, which is why they were picked, but nothing in the spec pins the roomy side.
  • Ring's 2×1 (156×108) is S narrow by the tier formula (W<240) even though Fable's table discusses it inside the "S wide" row alongside 3×1+; captioned here per the literal tier math, not the table's grouping.
  • Simplified the canvas-based overflow/fit-check machinery rev F used (fitSpan / runFitCheck) to plain CSS ellipsis, since every hero number here is hardcoded from the spec rather than derived at runtime — no live overflow detector was needed to hit the acceptance test's "no mock overflows its frame" bar, but it means this page can't self-flag a future data change the way rev D/F could.
