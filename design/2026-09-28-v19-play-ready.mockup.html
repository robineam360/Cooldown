1 · About card: Privacy policy

The row sits below “Share feedback” and above the existing unofficial disclaimer. It opens the same public policy in both flavors. No sign-in or network call is needed to see the row; a browser handles the link.

2 · Next launch: crash report card

One app-wide card sits directly below the account tabs and above usage cards. The existing “Checked…” status line and any usage error notice stay beneath the usage cards. This placement also works when the current account is signed out or has no data. The card never shows a stack trace or an email on screen.

StateVisible behavior
After Share reportThe share chooser opens with a scrubbed text report. On return, the card is gone. The local report remains available through the existing diagnostics path until its retention limit.
After Not nowThe card disappears and stays dismissed on later launches for this report.
Two crashes on fileOne card for the newest crash, with “2 reports on this phone.” Share report shares the newest report; older reports stay in diagnostics.
Exit reason, no traceThe card says a report could not be captured. There is no Share report action, since a trace-free report would mislead. Not now remains.

3 · Debug: Crash now

In Settings → More, tapping the version seven times reveals Diagnostics and Debug. “Crash now” is last in Debug, separated from synthetic data tools. It opens a confirmation dialog before the intentional crash; the next launch then shows the card in section 2. This entry is absent while locked.

4 · Play channel: no Updates card or update action

GitHub retains the Updates card and its manual check. Play removes that entire section; Polling and Usage credits meet the About section directly at narrow widths. The invalid-response notice still explains the failure, but has no “Check for updates” action in Play. Other notice actions keep their current behavior.

5 · Settings tabs: readable at 360 dp and 2.0× font

Proposed fix: one horizontally scrollable row at every width, with each title at full text size. No title is shortened or ellipsized. The selected tab scrolls into view when tapped or reached by a page swipe. At 360 dp, the next tab peeks in to indicate horizontal movement; users can still swipe the pages below. The row is 48 dp high normally and grows to at least 72 dp at 2.0× font, rather than clipping text.

6 · Widget reconfiguration label

The approved widget rev D already draws “Widget settings” with a “Save changes” button for long-press reconfiguration. The current code chooses that label only when saved widget preferences exist. When a launcher placed the widget before configuration, that check fails. The v1.9 fix restores the approved view for a placed widget, including that edge case; no new visual design is proposed here.

7 · Decisions requested in this review

Approve or revise the card position, the no-trace state, the “Crash now” confirmation, the Privacy policy row placement, and the scrollable Settings tabs. “Save changes” follows the previously approved widget rev D. The single-account notification wording follows the already approved Duet wording.
