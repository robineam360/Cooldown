Degraded Store Notice

What the app says when CCBG-46 (Keystore Wedge) runs a session on the empty in-memory store, in every state, at 360 dp (narrow phone), about 420 dp (Fold 7 cover) and about 750 dp (Fold 7 inner). Frames are 1 CSS px per dp; text is in sp at font scale 1.0 unless a frame says 2.0×. Usage values and times are sample data. New things are outlined in a dashed amber line; everything else is drawn from today's code.

Approved by Robin, 1 October 2026 · rev B · calls 1–7 all as drawn

Rev B, compared with rev A: a fresh Fable judge checked rev A against the code and found two things that would have misled you (section 7). Try again could have wiped the file it promises is untouched, so it now re-checks without reset rights. And a store whose reset failed had no wording, so it now has its own. The second wording no longer claims to know storage "still" fails. The restart card goes by each account's last change and has a full title for every case. The notes on the notification, the widgets and the shortcuts are corrected. There is an optional card for a store that was reset (call 7).

The policy, fixed in code (Astra C4-1, 2026-09-30). While the store can't be opened, the app runs on an empty store in memory and leaves the file alone. If nothing has been written yet, it keeps checking: whenever a poll or the app touches the store, at least five minutes apart, and again whenever the app's main screen is created (an open, a fold or unfold) and switches back to the real store as soon as it opens. Once a sign-in or sign-out is written to the memory store, the process stays on it until it ends. At the next restart that write is gone. It is never merged into the file and never overwrites it, so the sign-ins saved before come back. The notice has to say exactly that, and nothing more hopeful.
The page follows your system theme. This control only switches the device frames.

1 · The notice

An ongoing problem, so it takes the look of today's error notice (the amber "invalid response" row), not the neutral crash card. It is the same component with one plain-language body line added, because a status code alone can't explain this one. It can't be dismissed while the store is degraded, since it is the only thing that explains why your accounts are missing.

2 · Main screen

While degraded, no account has a token, so today the screen falls back to the first account's "No Personal account yet" card, which tells you to sign in. That advice is wrong here: your sign-in is very likely still saved, just locked. While the notice shows, that empty-account card is left out, so the notice is the whole page. Once you sign in anyway, the account gets its page and the notice changes to its second wording.

During the session

The next restart

If a change was held in memory, the app keeps a small record of it in ordinary settings: for each account, its key, its last change (a sign-in or a sign-out) and when the first change was made. Never a token. A sign-in then a Clear on one account counts as a sign-out, and two sign-ins count as one. On the next launch where the store opens, a one-time card says what was undone. It takes the crash card's look (neutral, past news) with an "i" for the "!", in the same slot. OK dismisses it; Open Settings dismisses it and opens Accounts. If the store still can't be opened on that launch, you get the notice from the start again and the record waits for a launch that works. A restart after a session where nothing was written shows nothing at all.

TimeThe crash card's rule: "today at 9:52", "yesterday at 21:07", otherwise "on 28 Sep".

Build rules this card depends on. (1) A Clear made while degraded does not touch the usage cache. Today it resets the plan, the sign-in flags and the auth state, and those would be wrong once the file brings the token back. (2) At the launch that drops a held change, any account left without a token has its whole cached snapshot cleared (usage, auth state, plan and email that a degraded sign-in wrote). Otherwise the widgets and the notification would keep a dropped sign-in's numbers with nothing to refresh them.

Other widths and sizes

3 · Settings → Accounts

The same notice, under the "Accounts" label. Account cards keep their real state: while degraded every account reads "Not signed in", and signing in works as usual. Two small copy changes while degraded: Clear's confirmation line reads "Work signed out — until Cooldown restarts." instead of "Work signed out.", and Remove account can't finish (see below).

Remove account while degraded

Removing an account deletes its history and settings for good, but its saved sign-in sits in the locked file, which the policy says must not be touched. Removing it anyway would leave that token stranded in the file under a retired slot that nothing ever reads again. Proposed: the ⋮ menu's "Remove account" stays, but while degraded it opens a one-button dialog instead of the usual confirm.

4 · Debug: Simulate degraded store

Behind the 7-tap unlock, in both flavors, placed just above "Crash test" as the second-to-last Debug card, so Step 7 can walk every state without touching the Keystore. Memory only: the switch makes this process act as if the store had failed with a non-permanent error. It never touches the Keystore or the file, and it is forgotten when the process ends. The restart state can still be checked: sign in while simulating, then close the app from Recents; the next launch opens the real store and shows the one-time card. The card also shows the real store's state, which helps diagnose a real failure.

5 · Outside the app: no notice

The pinned notification, the widgets and the reset pings get no notice. They read the usage cache, not the store. Background polls skip accounts without a token, so nothing is recorded and the last numbers stay, with their old checked time. A widget with no cached numbers shows its usual sign-in face. The launcher shortcuts would vanish today, because they are republished on every open and list only accounts with a token. While degraded they are left as they were (§1). Step 7 checks the notification while degraded, which also shows whether it needs anything in a later version.

6 · For you to decide

  1. The notice: the amber error-notice look, with the wordings in §1, not dismissable while degraded. Try again re-checks without reset rights, so it never deletes anything (as drawn). The other option is the full app-open check, which can wipe an unreadable file; the body would then have to drop "untouched".
  2. The empty-account card: leave out "No Personal account yet" while the notice shows (as drawn), or keep it under the notice.
  3. The restart card: a one-time neutral card naming what was undone, by each account's last change, with the wordings in §2 and OK · Open Settings (as drawn; the judge agrees, since it is the only thing that explains a sign-in that vanished). Or nothing at all on restart.
  4. Remove account while degraded: a one-button "can't remove now" dialog (as drawn), or let it remove everything else and leave the token stranded in the file.
  5. The Debug card: a switch in Debug just above Crash test, memory only, in both flavors.
  6. Outside the app: leave the notification and widgets as they are for v1.9, and keep the shortcuts as they were while degraded (as drawn).
  7. Ride-along, optional: when CCBG-46 does reset an unreadable store, today you only see "No Personal account yet", with no word that your sign-ins were deleted. The same one-time card could say so (drawn last in §2), or leave it out of v1.9.
