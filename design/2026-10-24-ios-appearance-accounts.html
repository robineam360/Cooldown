iOS Settings: Appearance, theme colour and account extras from Android

The features you picked from the parity list: group A (Appearance), B (Accounts) and C (Theme colour). The controls and wording are Android's, laid out as iOS Settings rows. Drawn at real size on an iPhone. iPad uses the same rows inside its Settings sheet.

A + C · Appearance, with theme colour

Android's order. The four new rows are Time format, Usage display, Chart orientation and Reset time, plus the red-past-pace switch and the Theme colour grid. Theme, Density and Chart height already exist on iOS. “Material You” is left out (Android only). Each choice applies everywhere: app, widgets, lock screen and Live Activity.

What the choices change: one 5h card

B · Accounts

Touch and hold an account in Settings → Accounts. The menu gains Details…, Accent colour… and Clear usage history…, as Android's ⋮ menu has them. Credits get their own switches, one per account that reports credits, as on Android.

Notes for approval. (1) Theme colour recolours every account at once. “Per provider” (default) keeps Claude orange and ChatGPT green. An account's own Accent colour overrides the theme colour, exactly as on Android. (2) Usage display: Left changes the words and figures (“53% left”), never the bars: bars and colours always show what's spent. (3) Time down turns the trend chart so usage runs left to right like the bar above it, and time runs top to bottom. (4) Turning red past the pace mark off keeps the tick; only the red fill goes.
