Lock-screen circle: no ring, both windows

The small round lock-screen widget (the one showing “5 · 5h” in your screenshot). iOS gives it a fixed round tile of about 72 pt and tints it white, so it can only use size, weight and bars, not colour. Each option below is shown at real size, then at 2× so you can read it. They all show both windows. States across each row: everyday, near the limit, at 100%, weekly-only plan, sign-in needed.

A · Two numbers stacked

5h big on top, Weekly under a hairline. Closest to the Number widget.

B · Two numbers, each with a bar

As A, plus a short bar with its pace tick under each number. Most information, but tightest.

C · Two upright bars

A tall bar per window, with its number beside it. Reads like a small chart.

Recommendation: A. At 72 pt on a lock screen, two numbers are what you can read at a glance. B’s bars are 3–4 pt tall at real size, too thin to read. The bars and pace ticks stay in the rectangular lock-screen widget beside it. That widget can also show both windows if you want; say so.
