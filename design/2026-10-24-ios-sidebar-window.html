iPad sidebar: 5h / Weekly switch, and evened-out marks

CCRM-94 (iOS App), from your notes on 24 Oct. Drawn at real size on the iPad landscape sidebar (300 pt wide).

1 · The switch

A two-way switch under the title picks which window the sidebar's bars and figures show. The choice is remembered. An account with no 5h window always shows Weekly, and its figure is labelled “Weekly” so the row doesn't look like a 5h reading. The switch fits in the sidebar, so it needs no Settings entry. The pinned and selected panes on the right don't change.

2 · Claude and ChatGPT marks at the same apparent size

Same nominal size today, but the Claude starburst runs edge to edge in its square while the ChatGPT knot fills about 70% of it, so Claude reads ~1.4× bigger. The fix draws the Claude mark smaller inside the same box, everywhere a mark appears (app, widgets, Live Activity). Android uses the same two marks and would get the same correction.
