iOS widgets — rev C

CCRM-94 (iOS App), from the iPad device check on 24 Oct. Layout by Fable; drawn at real point sizes (1 px = 1 pt) on the iPad 11″ canvas Robin runs (Display Zoom “More Space”: Small 160, Medium 346×157, Large 346×346, Extra Large 724×346). The Small and Medium faces you liked (Number, Countdown, Strip) don’t change.

What changes. (1) The small Ring goes; a new small Bars face shows one account’s 5h and Weekly. (2) The Overview (Large, Extra Large) loses its rings and becomes one account card drawn in five tiers, chosen by how much room each account gets. Extra Large always uses columns, so no bar runs wider than 420 pt. (3) With room to spare, the card carries the app’s own trend chart (decision D2). Every bar keeps its pace tick and the red overshoot from the tick.

What we’re fixing (your screenshot)

1 · Small “Bars” — replaces the small Ring

The Strip squeezed into a square: the 5h block on top, Weekly under it. Picks one account, like Number and Countdown. The reset line follows the footnote rule every face uses (sign in › as of › limit › runs out › resets).

Light, and where space runs out

Below 136 pt tall the Weekly reset line goes; below 119 pt the 5h reset line goes too and its clock moves into the header. iPhone SE (141 pt widget, 109 pt inside) is the tightest case.

3 · Extra Large (iPad) — a grid, never one wide column

One account gets its 5h and its Weekly side by side, each with its chart. Two accounts get a full card each, which is your case. More accounts move to narrower and shorter tiers.

4 · Medium Overview — new, only if the Ring goes (D1)

“All accounts” in the Medium size, in the Strip’s bar language. One account looks like the Strip.

5 · States on the account card

Large, three accounts in T4, then one account in T1 for each state. The chart never hides: with no reading it draws its guides and pace line, dimmed.

Decisions for you

D1 — The Ring widget goes entirely (recommended), or stays as a Medium only. Going adds the Medium Overview (section 4). At two accounts a Medium Ring puts two 80 pt rings at the quarter marks of a 346 pt widget, the same floating band as today’s Large. Rings stay where iOS needs them: the lock-screen circular and the Dynamic Island.
D2 — Trend chart on the roomiest cards (one account in a Large, up to three in an Extra Large): yes (recommended). It’s the app’s own chart, unchanged, and it’s the only data that fills that space with meaning. Caveat: iOS records history only when the app or a background refresh runs, so a young 5h curve may have just two or three points. The guides, pace, now and projection still draw. If no: those cards become T3 with a bigger number, and the space stays empty.
D3 — Too many accounts: a “+N more · open Cooldown” footer after six rows (Large), nine cells (Extra Large) or three (Medium) (recommended), or no cap (keep shrinking). Below a Mini, a bar loses its tick, and the tick is the point of the bar.
