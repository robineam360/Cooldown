    7 · App icon

    Robin asks whether the launcher icon should reflect the new provider. Same geometry as ic_launcher_foreground.xml and ic_launcher_background.xml (Pulse, CCRM-60 (Dual Identity) round 10), drawn at 192 px and at 48 px, the true launcher size. The iOS AppIcon is the same art (one 1024 px image, no mask crop). Recommendation: V1, keep it. The Pulse is the app’s identity, not a provider list: the trace is full from x 12 to 96, so a third provider has no honest place on it, and V2 recolours "even pace", the one element that is not an account.

    8 · Theme colour

    Gemini Blue already exists on Android as Gemini’s provider colour, provider-only and outside both grids, exactly like ChatGPT Green (foundations.md §1.2). Recommendation: option A, no change: Gemini accounts default to Gemini Blue through "Per provider", as ChatGPT ones default to ChatGPT Green. Option B offers both provider colours in both grids.
