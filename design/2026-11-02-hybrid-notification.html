2 · States (H3 and H4, side by side)

The ring follows the same honesty rules as the status-bar ring (spec §4.3): accent up to 80%, then the ladder colour; a red slice from the needle to the fill tip when used is more than 3 points ahead of the clock; hairline only with no reading; spent cross at 100%. It draws the spend in every Usage display mode; only the text beside it (H3) or in the bore (H4) follows Used, Left or Vs pace. Each state is drawn twice, H3 then H4. H4 differs only in the ring itself: hollow, 44 dp, the number inside, no hub (the bore is for the number), no spent cross (it would sit under the digits: at 100% the arc is full, the red step, and the bore says "100%").

3 · Expanded, one account

Proposal: the ring stays in the header, and the panel's companion row stays too. They do different jobs: the ring is the glance in the same place as collapsed (so expanding never moves it), and the row is the detail the ring cannot hold: its reset line ("Resets in 2h 20m · 5:10 pm"), its own bar and tick, and the 5h projection. Dropping the row would hand back 43 dp, and nothing needs it. The header text beside the ring drops to the number alone when it would only repeat the row's. Left as drawn here (number repeated) so you can judge it. H4, same proposal: the 44 dp ring stays at the left of the header block, which is 71 dp tall expanded, so it is centred with 13.5 dp spare above and below and sits exactly where collapsed put it (measured, below). The bore carries the number, so there is no text repeat to trim; the panel's 5h row still holds the reset line and projection.

4 · The Duet (two accounts, 156 dp per half)

Collapsed halves are two-line blocks with no sub line (spec §2.2), so there is no text to carry a number. Proposal: a bare 14 dp ring at the right end of the label line (14 dp high), no digits. 14 dp is the status-bar ring's true rendered size, so the geometry is already proven. It costs the label ceiling 18 dp (138 → 120 dp; with one dot 127 → 109; with two 116 → 98). The bar and the figure are untouched, so a half stays 52 dp. An account with no 5h (Personal) shows no ring. Each ring follows its own account's Headline: Weekly lead → 5h ring, 5h lead → Weekly ring. H4 in the Duet: a 36 dp ring on the left of the half (bore "34%" at 9.8 sp, no tiny label, too small to read), label + bar + a 28 sp figure on the right. It is the expensive one: Teams' bar falls from 86 to 47 dp (measured) while Personal, with no 5h, keeps the full row. Narrow rule: a half under about 150 dp (the Fold 7 inner screen's 101 dp) cannot take it, so the half uses H3's bare 14 dp label ring, and that drops under the 60 dp label rule exactly as in rev A.

5 · iOS: the Live Activity and the Dynamic Island

iOS has no persistent notification; the Live Activity is the nearest surface. Same idea: the Weekly bar leads, the 5h ring replaces the thin "5h" line (a thin line plus a 5 pt bar, in spec §2.7 and the v2.0 section). Dark palette, #1C1C1E at 86%, 16 pt horizontal and 12 pt vertical padding, as shipped. SwiftUI draws the ring with the same Canvas path as the widgets' ring face, so no new renderer. H4 on iOS: the same hollow ring at the left of the row (44 pt on the Lock Screen, 36 pt in the Duet, 30 pt compact Island, 48 pt expanded Island), the Weekly stack on the right. 7 · Open questions

Each with the recommendation I would take if you say "go".

  1. Placement. H1, H2, H3 or H4? Recommend H4 (rev B: ring on the left, 44 dp, bore number) for single accounts and 156 dp Duet halves, H3's 14 dp ring as the narrow Duet fallback; H3 alone remains the safe choice if the shorter Weekly bar (248 → 190 dp) bothers you. Reasons in §1.
  2. Digits beside the ring. H3: single layout carries "5h 34%" as text; the Duet halves have none. Recommend keep the text on single and none on the Duet (the ring alone is a glance; the expanded panel has the number). H4: the number is inside the ring everywhere, so there is no separate text.
  3. Auto takeover. Ring swaps to Weekly while 5h leads. The alternative is the ring staying 5h, duplicating the bar. Recommend the swap. H4: the ring stays in its left slot and swaps content (bar and figure 5h, ring Weekly), drawn in Q3 and X2; the other option (ring 5h, bar Weekly) hides the takeover.
  4. Weekly spent. Dim the 5h ring to 45% (drawn, both variants) or drop it as rev A's E3 dropped the companion. Recommend dim: it keeps the row's shape and CCRM-102 (Spent Week Gate) already treats 5h as moot then.
  5. A setting? A "Show 5h ring" switch under "Always-on notification". Recommend none, in the spirit of CCRM-61 (Settings Diet); no 5h on the account already means no ring. Revisit only if someone asks.
  6. Expanded duplication. The panel's 5h row repeats what the header ring says. Recommend keep both for rev A, since the row alone carries the reset line and projection.
  7. Vs pace text. The ring text is the distance ("+19%"), the ring itself the spend. Recommend this, consistent with the panel rows.
  8. Narrow Duet. Drop the ring when the label ceiling would fall under 60 dp (a long label plus a condition dot at 101 dp). Conditions dots keep priority over the ring. Recommend this rule; at 101 dp with short labels it still fits (D4).
  9. H4: tiny label in the bore. A 7 sp "5h" (and "Weekly" when the ring is the Weekly) under the number at 44 dp; at 36 dp (Duet) and 30 pt (Island) it is dropped as unreadable, leaving the ring's window implied by the Headline. Recommend keep it at 44 dp and up, drop below; the alternative is no tiny label anywhere, which saves the crowding at "100%" (about 1.9 dp clear of the arc at 11 sp) but loses the cue on Auto takeover.
  10. H4: the Weekly figure. It stays 32 sp (66 dp wide) and the bar takes the loss (190 dp). Recommend keep 32 sp; drop to 28 sp (≈ +8 dp bar) only if the device pass shows the bar crowded. On iOS the Lock Screen figure goes 52 → 44 pt so the text stack fits, already drawn.
  11. H4: the Duet. The 36 dp ring cuts Teams' bar to 47 dp while Personal keeps 86 dp, so the two halves' bars differ. Recommend accept for halves of 150 dp and up, H3's 14 dp ring below; if the mismatch looks wrong on the device, use H3's 14 dp ring on every Duet half and keep H4 for the single layouts only.
  12. Status-bar glyph. Stays one ring. Recommend no change; a wide twin glyph would shrink to unreadable (spec §4.1).
  13. Implementation cost. The ring is a second bitmap (RemoteViews cannot host a drawable ring), drawn by the existing RingRenderer; one extra ImageView per account in the layouts. Worth confirming before build that the extra bitmap per poll does not push the notification past its update budget. Not a design question, flagged for the plan.
