1 · Brief and where the concepts came from

Robin asked for a better launcher icon. The brief: it is about hitting the weekly usage limit; Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini are all present, by colour only, never logos; professional; legible at 48 px; a working monochrome themed icon (Android 13); it survives circle, squircle and rounded-square masks; and the same art goes on iOS.

Both concepts are ChatGPT's (GPT, through the model-mesh reviewer), given as exact geometry on the 108-unit canvas. It judged today's Pulse as concerns:

"The described Pulse suggests heart monitoring rather than a usage allowance" and "V1 omits a required service" (Gemini). Weekly vs daily "cannot" be told from the symbol alone, so the app's headline carries the period.

Drawn below from those numbers, not redrawn by taste. The 72-unit safe zone is outlined faintly on each 192 px canvas. Masked and iOS views show the central 72 units, as a launcher does. Squircle is approximated by a 34% radius.
