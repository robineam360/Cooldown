1 · The app

Default card order becomes Weekly, then 5h; a layout you reordered keeps your order. iOS Comfortable opens the first card and folds the second on iPhone (both open on iPad), so the Weekly card now opens first. The Weekly halves (All, Fable) each get the pace line and the signed-Pace detail line; the 5h card's "below even pace" line goes and its projection line stays; the folded 5h line reads the projection.

iPad sidebar: the figure follows the Headline

The sidebar's remembered 5h / Weekly switch gains a first segment, Headline (the new default): each row shows its own account's headline window, tagged with that window's name. Weekly and 5h keep today's behaviour exactly (a no-5h plan shows Weekly, tagged).

2 · Settings: the Headline, per account

iOS has no per-account page today (a row, and a touch-and-hold menu). The row now pushes a small page with Plan, Accent colour (the menu's Accent colour… still works) and the new Headline group. Alternative if you'd rather not add a page: a "Headline…" item in the touch-and-hold menu opening this group as a sheet.
