CCRM-99 (Model Limits Toggle) · rev A · 2026-11-02 · Android and iPhone/iPad

Hide the model limits on an account

Your ask: an account-level switch that turns off the per-model weekly limits (Claude's Fable, ChatGPT's Spark), because you rarely use those models and Weekly, then 5h, is what matters. Default: shown, so nothing changes until you turn it off. Hiding only stops Cooldown drawing them: the limit still applies to that model, and the caption says so.
