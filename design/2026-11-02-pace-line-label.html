Where the pace mark sits, and how far you are from it

Your ask: "my usage is 50% while the pace line is at 60%, so I have 10% more breathing space", and the reverse, 80% against a 60% mark. Every bar already draws the pace mark; none says where it is. Three ways to say it. All use points (60 − 50 = 10), the plain distance on the bar, and the words "pace mark", as the existing setting "Show red past the pace mark" does.

One knock-on, recommended with every variant: the Weekly card's detail line from rev A, "Pace +21% · an even week is 39% by now", goes. The distance in points replaces it, so the app never shows two different "% from pace" numbers. The signed Pace still sorts the Summary list and picks the band word; it just isn't printed.

Examples: A, under: 50% used, mark at 60%, 2.8 days left: "Room to spare · ≈18%/day". B, over: 80% used, mark at 60%: "Slow down · ≈7%/day".

V1 · A caption at the mark recommended

One 11.5 sp line under the bar, starting at the pace mark, so it points at what it names: "60% pace mark · 10 under" or "· 20 over", beginning just right of the mark so the mark itself points at it. When there isn't room to the right of the mark, it ends just left of it instead. Under pace, the gap between your fill and the mark is drawn as a dashed outline: the room you have left. Over pace, the existing red slice is that gap. Costs 22 dp per bar.

V2 · In the title row, next to the figure

No new line, no new marks: the title row reads "50% of 60% pace". It is the cheapest, but you subtract it yourself, and the number is far from the mark it describes.

V3 · A flag on the mark

A small flag above the mark carries the mark's value; the distance goes in the pace words ("Room to spare · 10 under · ≈18%/day"). The flag reads well, but it collides with the title row's figure whenever the mark is near the right end, and that happens every week, near the reset.

V1 on every surface it touches

The 5h bars too: the mark is plain geometry, not the Pace verdict. Summary rows take the caption in place of nothing (the pip stays). The notification has no height for a caption, so its expanded header's second line carries it: "10 under the pace mark (60%) · resets Fri". The collapsed row is unchanged. Widgets are unchanged: they have no room, and the tick is already there.
