CCRM-98 (Summary Tab) · rev A · 2026-11-02 · Android and iPhone/iPad

Summary: every account's week on one page, and which to use next

Your three answers: an "All" tab first, opened at launch when there are two or more accounts (a toggle turns that off); "Use next" picks the account with the most unused quota for its time left, skipping any whose 5h is binding or whose week is spent; and a Pace map, which plots every account against its own week on a single chart. Everything reuses CCRM-97 (Pace Number): the same bands, the same words and the same allowance. The pace tick on each bar is neutral ink, the existing rule for any face that shows more than one account.

Fixtures: Wednesday 2:50 pm. Teams (Claude Max), Weekly 52%, Sun 9:30 pm, 5h 34% · Product (Claude Pro, blue accent), Weekly 30%, resets Fri 1:50 pm, 5h 93%, back 3:40 pm · Personal (ChatGPT Pro, no 5h), Weekly 50%, resets Fri 10:18 pm · Side (Claude Pro, purple), spent, back Thu 8:00 am.

1 · The rule, in one table

  1. Eligible: a reading exists, the week is not spent, and the 5h is not binding (BEHAVIOR-SPEC §6.2).
  2. Ranked by weekly Pace, lowest first: the most quota that would otherwise go unused. A tie goes to the earlier reset. The first eligible account is Use next.
  3. Then the rest, in this order: held by a binding 5h (soonest back first), spent (soonest reset first), no reading.
  4. When a held account would rank above Use next, the card says so: "Product has more to spare once its 5h is back at 3:40 pm."
  5. Nobody eligible: the card names the soonest way back instead of a pick.
