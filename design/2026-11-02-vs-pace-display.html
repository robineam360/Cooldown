CCRM-100 (Vs Pace Display) · rev A · and "5h first" for CCRM-96 (Weekly First) · 2026-11-02

The big figure as distance from the pace mark

Your option A: Settings › Appearance › Usage display gains a third value: Used · Left · Vs pace. In Vs pace every big figure shows the pace mark minus usage, in points: "+10%" means 10 under the mark (room), "−20%" means 20 over. It is rounded down, so room is never overstated. Bars, ticks and colours don't change: they still key on usage, so a "+5%" drawn in orange means on pace but 92% used. The pace-mark caption (V1, chosen) flips to show what the figure no longer does, the usage. Mocks use your Huge number style. Only the figure and its caption change.

2 · Notification

The figure becomes the distance. The caption slot under it, which reads "LEFT" in Left mode, reads VS PACE. The sub line and the panel rows are rev A's, except that each panel row's own figure is a distance too. The expanded line 2, where V1's distance went in Used mode, carries the usage instead.

3 · App cards and Summary rows

The card figure gets a small "vs pace" word after it, in the slot where Left mode puts "left". The V1 caption under the bar reads "52% used · mark 39%". The Summary keeps its order (Pace still ranks), and its figures become distances.

4 · Widgets, the gauge and iOS

Every figure in a ring bore, Number face, iPad sidebar row or lock-screen circular follows the token, as Left already does. The status-bar gauge has no figure, so nothing changes there. The faces' sizes, rings and drop rules are as shipped; "+10%" and "−20%" are no wider than "100%", so no fit changes.

6 · Keeping 5h first, for those who prefer it

Weekly leads by default, and 5h first stays one switch away, per account: the account's Headline set to 5-hour puts 5h back at the top of everything it touches. That covers the notification, the status-bar gauge and new widgets, and, new here, the app's card order: the 5h card sits above Weekly, as today, unless you've reordered cards in the layout sheet, where your order wins. Existing users also get a one-time notice after the update, so nobody is surprised.
