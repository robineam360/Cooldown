Weekly first, and the Pace in words

Your six decisions of 2 Nov, drawn on every surface they touch. Weekly leads, and 5h comes along small. Pace on weekly-length windows reads as a band plus a daily allowance, "Slow down · ≈11%/day". The Headline setting is per account (Weekly · 5-hour · Auto). The status-bar gauge shows Weekly until 5h is the thing about to stop you. Notification mocks use your Huge number style. Nothing on any card is removed: the bars keep their pace tick and red slice, and the chart keeps its guides, diagonal, dots, projection and axis.

Fixtures (one moment, used everywhere): Wednesday 2:50 pm. Teams (Claude Max): Weekly 52%, resets Sun 9:30 pm (39% of the week gone); 5h 34%, resets 5:10 pm; Fable 30%. Personal (ChatGPT Pro, no 5h window): Weekly 50%, resets Fri 10:18 pm (67% gone).

1 · The words: one ladder, every surface

pace = (time left − quota left) ÷ max(time left, quota left) × 100, in −100…+100. Only on weekly-length windows: Weekly and the weekly model caps (Fable, Spark). The band is the word. The allowance is quota left ÷ days left, which is the even rate that lands on exactly 100% at the reset. The signed number appears in one place only, the app card's detail line (§5).

Why the allowance switches to hours on the last day: "40% ≈ 160%/day" is true but useless with 6 hours left. Under 24 h the clause becomes "40% left, 6h". Why there is no "too early" floor: unlike today's projection, Pace is calm at a window's start (2% used two hours in reads as on pace), so the weekly line never goes silent while a reading exists.

2 · Pinned notification, one account, Huge number

Collapsed: the title is the headline window ("Teams · Weekly"), the bar and figure are Weekly, and the one line reads band · allowance · 5h companion. Expanded: the header gets two lines (pace, then the weekly reset), and the panel's first row becomes the 5h row (it used to be the Weekly row). The model-cap rows and condition strips are unchanged. With no 5h on the account, the companion becomes "resets Sun".

Where 5h matters: the companion turns loud, or (on Auto) takes the headline

5h at 93%, back at 3:40 pm. With the Headline on Weekly (default), the companion takes the bar colour's severity and bold, so it can't be missed. With the Headline on Auto, the 5h becomes the headline until it resets. The rule is: 5h past 90%, or projected to hit 100% before its reset (unless the week is spent).

3 · Duet, two accounts

The structure is unchanged; what changes is which window sits where. Collapsed: both halves show Weekly, with no sub line, as today. Expanded: each header block is Weekly, with its sub "band · allowance · resets day". The panel's rows become the 5h rows (an account with no 5h, like ChatGPT Pro, has none). The panel budget is unchanged: two rows take the 78 dp the two Weekly rows took, so CCBG-26 (Panel Scaling)'s one-strip cap still holds.

4 · Status-bar gauge (and the desktop menu-bar item later)

The gauge geometry is untouched (CCRM-83 (Ring Renderer): extent, usage, red slice, hub, needle, spent ×). Only its input changes: Weekly, until 5h blocks. The needle is the clock of whichever window it draws, so a takeover shows as the needle jumping from Wednesday's position to "5h nearly over". By the rule, 5h is always past 90% (orange or red) or on course to run out when it takes over. That colour is the signal, and the notification title names the window. No extra glyph fits in ~14 dp, and none is proposed. If the account's Headline is set to 5-hour, the gauge shows 5h throughout.

5 · The app: Weekly card first

The default card order becomes Weekly, then 5h, and a layout you have reordered in the sheet keeps your order. The Weekly card's first line under the chart, today "40% below even pace", becomes band · allowance until the reset day. A new detail line carries the signed Pace with what it is measured against. The projection line and the reset row are unchanged. The chips switch the chart and the pace lines between All and Fable, as today.

Question for you (5h's line). The 5h window gets no Pace (decision 2), so "22% below pace" there would be the old gap metric beside the new one. Recommended: 5h shows the projection instead. Folded, that reads "Resets in 2h 20m · on track for ~69%". Open, the "below even pace" line goes and the existing "At this pace: ~69% when the window resets" line stays. The alternative is the band word with no number, "Resets in 2h 20m · under pace". Both are drawn above, the recommended one in the cards.

6 · Settings: Headline, per account

The setting sits in the account's own page (Settings › Accounts › the account), under its colour, and reads "what the notification, status-bar gauge, tile and new widgets lead with". On an account with no 5h window it is shown but greyed out, with its reason.

8 · What changes in spec/core

  • New §6.1 Pace number: formula, the five bands and the spent state, the allowance, the switch to hours under 24 h, the suppressions (no reset, not started, no reading), and fixtures: the six ModelMesh scenarios plus window start, exhausted, reset reached and every band edge.
  • §5 dead zone: the 3-point gap verdict is retired on weekly-length windows in favour of the ±5 band. The chart's amber wash past the diagonal is geometry and stays.
  • §1 headline rule: "session if present, else weekly" becomes the Headline setting. Weekly is the default, 5-hour is literal, and the takeover rule applies to Auto and the gauge. An absent 5h always means Weekly.
