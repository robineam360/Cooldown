3 · Fetch-error and expiring notices on the main screen needs approval

Android: seven fetch-error kinds, each with a title, an evidence line and an action, drawn beside the last-good cards (S/app.md:195-209). iOS today: plan, sign-in, stale and Gemini's "Google changed something" only; a network or server error shows nothing but a footer time, so a user cannot tell "Anthropic is down" from "Cooldown is broken". Change: the same NoticeView (14 pt radius, orange or red tint, capsule button), Android's copy with the vendor named, and an "expires soon" notice (BEHAVIOR-MOBILE §3: within 7 days). Last-good cards stay and dim to 45%, as for sign-in today.

Rev B decision · section 3 (★ taken, tint override)

  • One notice at a time, same NoticeView. Precedence: plan refusal › sign-in needed › last fetch’s error (Gemini’s “Google changed something” first for an invalid reply; else rate-limited, network, server, invalid reply, internal) › stale (> 6 h) › expiring (Claude, ≤ 7 d). A fetch-error notice absorbs stale: its evidence line appends “ · last reading Thu 9:50 pm”.
  • Kinds (tone · title · evidence · button): rate-limited · warning · “Rate limited — backing off, retries on its own.” · message + “next try ~3:40 pm” · none. network · warning · “Couldn’t reach <Vendor> — check your connection, or see if it’s them.” · message · “Check <Vendor> status”. server · warning · “<Vendor>’s server errored — usually theirs, usually brief.” · “HTTP 503” · “Check <Vendor> status”. invalid reply · warning · “The server answered in a shape this app doesn’t know — an app update may be needed.” · message · none. internal · red · “Something unexpected went wrong in the app.” · message · none. expiring · quiet · “Sign-in expires in 2d 3h” + “Sign in again to keep polling going.” · “Sign in again”.
  • Status button opens the provider’s status page in Safari. Evidence lines 12.5 pt monospaced; no body text on the five error kinds (as Android).
  • Tint override: “quiet” notices use the grey card fill, not orange. This also recolours the plan-refusal notice from orange to grey (it is not a failure) — a ride-along, called out here so it can be reversed.
  • Dimming unchanged: last-good cards dim to 45% under error or warning only. A first fetch that failed shows the notice alone. 320 pt: title wraps, button hugs its text.

Rev A options · section 3 · settled in the box above

  1. One notice at a time in that order. Yes; or stack the two most important (Android's notification does up to three).
  2. Invalid-reply notice on a TestFlight build: Android offers "Check for updates" on its GitHub build. No button; the body says "An app update may be needed."; or a button that opens the TestFlight app.
  3. "Check Anthropic status" opens the provider's status page in Safari (the three hosts already in the app). Yes.
