3 · Defects

D1 · One-line Summary row: a long name runs under the bar — medium

What I saw
With ten accounts (one-line rows) and the name “Consultancy”, the name's last letters sit under the bar's start dot. The glyph column has already left, as the rule says. The 17e, with the same data, shows a small gap.
Width
Folded Duo: one-line row 318 pt (page column 382). The name is .fixedSize() (“it never truncates on a one-line row”) and the bar has minWidth: 50, so any row narrower than 246 pt + the name's width overflows: 331 pt for “Consultancy”, which overflows by 13 pt here. ios/App/UI/Summary.swift ≈ l. 367–374.
Proposed fix
Once the glyph has left, let the name ellipsize down to its 64 pt floor (or let the bar go under 50 pt), so the row never overflows.
Changes what the user sees?
Yes, it needs a small wireframe. The approved I4 rule never covered a row narrower than its minimum, so which part gives first is a design choice.

D2 · Settings › Reset alerts: the window word is truncated away — medium (every narrow iPhone)

What I saw
Each account has two rows, “Teams · 5h” and “Teams · Weekly”. The segmented Off / If busy / Always control is .fixedSize() (≈ 186 pt), so the label truncates to “Teams ·…” on both rows, and “Personal” to “Persona…”. Only “Lab · 5h” fits, and “Lab · W…” already truncates. The two rows of an account cannot be told apart.
Width
Folded Duo (350 pt card) and iPhone 17e (358 pt card). Any name longer than about 4 characters truncates. ResetModeRow, ios/App/UI/SettingsHistory.swift ≈ l. 601–616.
Proposed fix
Name the account once (a sub-header or the first row) and label the two rows “5h” and “Weekly”, or put the control under the label.
Changes what the user sees?
Yes, it needs a wireframe.

D3 · Folded rail: account tabs lose their names — medium (needs a decision)

What I saw
On the folded Duo, iOS 27 draws the .sidebarAdaptable TabView as a vertical rail on the right with icons only. On the 17e the bottom bar has labels. Each account tab's icon is its provider mark, so Teams, Lab and Atlas (all Claude) show three identical ✳. With 10 accounts the rail also folds the toolbar into “•••”, so History and Settings each take one more tap, and the extra accounts sit under “•••” too.
Width
Folded Duo, 466 pt window. Not seen on the 17e.
Proposed fix
Decide how a tab names its account where the system hides labels: a monogram or accent-tinted mark per account, or another tab style on this device class.
Changes what the user sees?
Yes, it needs a wireframe. The chrome is the system's (“Native everywhere”, Robin 2026-11-02), so this may also be accepted as is.

D4 · History's account strip scrolls under the rail — low

What I saw
With four or more accounts the strip scrolls horizontally. Its scroll view spans the whole 466 pt window (frame 0–466), not the 382 pt column, so tabs slide under the status rail, and “Gemini” shows as “em” on the far side of the rail. The divider under the strip does stop at the column.
Width
Folded Duo. Not seen on the 17e (no side rail).
Proposed fix
Keep the strip's scroll view inside the safe area (no trailing safe-area bleed, so it clips at the column edge), like the rest of the page. AccountTabs, ios/App/UI/MainScreens.swift ≈ l. 196.
Changes what the user sees?
No. It restores the approved strip, which stays inside the page column.

4 · Questions

Q1 · The unfolded inner screen gets the phone page

Answered 2026-11-12 (U1): half yes. With the inner screen wide (951 pt), the app already shows the iPad page. With it tall (669 pt), it shows the phone page stretched to 637 pt, filed as CCBG-90 (Inner Portrait Stretch). Apple's guidance asks for a regular-width layout on the inner display in every pose, and warns against fixed width breakpoints (U6). The original question follows.

Inner screen ≈ 669 × 951 pt. TabPage.iPad needs a regular width of 700 pt or more, so whatever size class iOS reports, the unfolded Duo shows the phone page with cards about 637 pt wide. The account tile and chart then run about 280 pt wider than on any phone, which risks the “charts too wide” look. Should the threshold come down to about 660 pt, so the inner screen gets the iPad page (header and cards two across from 396 pt)? That is a wireframe question. It also needs a real unfolded pass, in the simulator panel or on hardware, to measure the size class.

U6 · Apple's guidance, and the ask for the inner screen

Apple's HIG page Designing for iPhone Duo (dated 2026-09-09), read 2026-11-12:

  • A compact-width layout on the outer display and a regular-width layout on the inner display “give you the fundamentals for every pose”.
  • Show “an additional level of hierarchy on the larger inner display if it makes sense”: split views expand there and collapse on the outer display (Mail: list on the cover screen, list plus message side by side inside).
  • “Avoid fixed widths and display-specific dependencies”: use size classes, layout margins and safe areas, not breakpoints. Cooldown's 600 / 700 / 900 / 1000 pt rules are exactly such breakpoints.
  • Bars move to a vertical strip on the side, except on the inner display held tall, which keeps horizontal bars. Both match what was measured.
  • Keep the state the same between displays. It is kept (U5).
  • Half open, the fold is a reserved region. Prefer an even number of columns so content divides cleanly, and “avoid extreme layout changes as people fold the device”.

Robin's starting ideas (2026-11-12, thinking out loud, not decided): rather than a bigger phone page, use the inner screen's space and the half-open pose:

  • Tall, half open (the fold runs across): the usage list in the top half, scrolling on its own, with the Pace map in the bottom half.
  • Wide, half open (the fold runs down the middle): usage bars on the left and pace on the right.
  • The same thinking applies to the flat inner screen in both orientations: an inner-screen layout of its own, not the phone design blown up.

That is a wireframe task (CLAUDE.md §2) covering CCBG-90 and probably CCBG-86, outside this check-and-report pass.

8 · How to repeat

  • The Duo needs its own runtime. The generic iOS 27.2 runtime (24B8117) lists iPhone19,4 as unsupported. xcodebuild -downloadPlatform iOS -buildVersion 27.1 fetched iOS-27-2-iPhone19-4 (24B91016s), about 8 GB. Then xcrun simctl create "iPhone Duo" com.apple.CoreSimulator.SimDeviceType.iPhone-Duo com.apple.CoreSimulator.SimRuntime.iOS-27-2-iPhone19-4.
  • Screenshots: xcrun simctl io <udid> screenshot --display=1 (cover); the inner screen is display 3.
  • Don't toggle display power with screenConfig: it crashed SpringBoard here (restrictedClassicModeDisplayConfiguration…), and only a reboot of the simulator fixed it.
  • Unfolded: open /Applications/Xcode.app/Contents/Applications/DeviceHub.app (Xcode 27.2 has no Window-menu item for it) and pick the Duo. The fold and unfold buttons are at the bottom of its window, holding ⌥ shows the hinge slider, and ⌘← / ⌘→ turns the device.
  • Window and size class: attach lldb to the app's pid (simctl spawn … launchctl list) and print the scene's effectiveGeometry and the key window's frame, size classes and safe area. End with process detach, or the batch exit kills the app.
  • On the inner screen XCUITest taps land but swipes do not, so ask for the scroll. Tap by element, not by coordinate: coordinates are off once the interface is in a landscape orientation.
