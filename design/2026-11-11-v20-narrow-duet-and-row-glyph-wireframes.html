F1 · A state word with no tail — as built (captures 31, 33)

§1's ladder is: the bar absorbs → the name ellipsizes to 24 dp → the word drops its tail. Green: 32 dp or more (about "Pers…"); amber: 24 to 32 ("Te…"); red: under 24. A word's width includes its 6 dp gap; the line is padded by that width + 4% (Duet.wordPadDp).

Variant A — short forms

Each long word has a short form, taken when the name would fall under 32 dp: "No reading" → "No data" (43 dp) → gone; "Not started" → "Idle" (28 dp) → gone; "Sign in" has none and stays, because it is the action. Cost: two words that exist nowhere else in the app, and it still fails where it matters — at 220 dp "No data" leaves 22 dp, so the word simply vanishes, and "Sign in" still crushes the name (24 dp at 220, 4 at 200).

Variant B — the word becomes the dot

Under 32 dp of name the word becomes v2.0's 6 dp dot in the word's colour, after the name (11 dp): fault red for "Sign in", grey for the other two; the full word lives in the expanded view. Name room is bar − 29 for every word. Cost: on a 360 dp phone the Duet is back to what §1 replaced, a dot that says "expand me", and "Sign in" is never printed there. If the row-glyph section settles a glyph set, B would draw those in a 12 dp box instead (bar − 36).

Variant C — the word takes the figure's place recommended

A row with no figure has nothing in its 42 dp slot but "—". On a shade under 256 dp of content (smallest width under 396 dp; 136 + 18 + 61 + 40 = 255 is where "Not started" stops leaving 40 dp of name) that row drops the dash: its name line runs on over the 8 dp gap and the slot, 50 dp further, to the row's right edge, and the word sits there, right-aligned where the figure would be. The bar keeps its length and its left edge. Name room is bar + 32 − the padded word: 74 dp beside "Sign in" and 56 beside "No reading" at 220. The rule keys on the screen, not on the name, so both rows of one notification behave alike; from 256 dp (the Fold 7 cover's 272 and wider) §1 stays exactly as approved. flag departs from §1's "Sign in / No reading / Not started: —" on narrow shades only.

Build note (F2). Column widths are derived from the screen's smallest width for both layouts; no fixed numbers. Content = Duet.contentDp(sw): sw − 140 below 600 dp, 352 from 600. Expanded content = content + 40, because the expander's column comes back (A2: 133 + 126 = 259 at 360, 194 + 126 = 320 at 420). Column = (content − ring box − gaps − the figure's own width) × 0.965, the 3.5% CCBG-78 (Collapsed Pace Line Clip) took off for the estimate's error. Ring box: 44 collapsed; expanded, the wider of 44 and the time line; none with no 5h. Gaps: 14 dp each side of the column, one with no ring. The figure's width is its em (bold, "%" at 58%) × its size in dp, so the column moves with the figure: 82 dp beside "−13%", 102 beside "52%". Sizes in dp come from the system (TypedValue.applyDimension), not sp × fontScale: Android 14 scales large text non-linearly (24 sp → 26.4 dp at 1.3 and 36 dp at 2.0; 32 sp stays 32 at 1.3), and with that table this model gives A2's four measured Duet columns (133, 194, ≈121, 61) within about 1 dp. The rule. Wherever the Summary is drawn (two or more accounts) the All · Personal · Work control is in its header and every row leads with its tag's glyph: a 14 dp glyph and 4 dp before the provider mark, 18 dp in all, one mark wide. Straight after the upgrade every glyph is the person, and the Work segment is disabled (38%) until an account is marked Work.
