1 · CCBG-84 (One-line Name Overrun) — Summary, ten accounts

A one-line Summary row (six or more accounts) is: rank disc 16, name 15 pt, bar 50–110, the word slot 96, the figure 52, gaps 8 — 246 pt before the name. On the folded iPhone Duo the card is 350 pt and the row 318, so a name has 72 pt. Today the name is never cut (.fixedSize()), so "Consultancy" (82 pt) pushes the row 10 pt over and its end runs under the bar. The glyph column has already left (the bar would have gone under 50), as the approved rule says.

Option A — once the glyph has left, the name ellipsizes; the bar keeps 50 pt

3 · New: Chart End Labels — the trend chart's right edge late in a heavy week

Found checking CCBG-95 (Snapshot Label Overlap) on iOS. iOS draws the 100% / 90% / 80% guide labels inside the plot at its right edge (Android has a gutter outside). At 95% used with 95% of the week gone, the "95%" figure butts into "100%" and the now-dot covers "90%". Every iOS trend chart: the open tile and the share card.

The shipped TrendChart, rendered at 3× (260 × 130 pt, the share card’s size): 95% at 95% of the week, ~100% projected.
Now (redrawn; red boxes): "95%"+"100%" touch; the dot covers "90%".
A · give way. A guide label is not drawn when it would come within 4 pt of the now figure, the now-dot or the projection label (the axis ordinals' give-way rule). The dashed lines stay, so 100 / 90 stay readable by colour. Elsewhere in the week nothing changes.
B · gutter, as Android. The plot ends 30 pt earlier and the three labels sit outside it, always shown. Every chart's plot gets narrower, all week.
Recommended: A. It only changes the frames that collide; B narrows every chart all week to fix the last day.
