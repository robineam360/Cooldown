Model share tint on the Weekly bar (Claude accounts)

Design for Robin, 2026-11-13 · wireframe for approval (CLAUDE.md §2). Robin's idea: draw the Fable cap inside the Weekly bar as a lighter leading segment, as Claude's own Settings › Usage does (design/research/2026-11-13-fable-tint/claude-settings-usage.webp: Weekly 88%, Fable 55% used, "Up to 50% of your weekly limit", lighter segment ≈ 27.5% of the bar), and drop the separate Fable row. It is an option on the existing "Show model limits" switch (CCRM-99 (Model Limits Toggle)). Drawn at 1 dp = 1 px (iOS 1 pt = 1 px), real numbers from spec/mobile/surfaces/app.md §2.4.4+§2.4.5 (the tile) and the iOS tile; panels marked dark or light are forced so both show. No tracker ID is allocated here; the brief is design only.

Recommendation in one paragraph. The share is not in the payload (§1), so the tint draws Fable % × an assumed 50%, clamped to the Weekly fill, flagged UNVERIFIED until Robin captures his own usage payload. The lighter segment leads, in the Weekly fill's own hue at 55% opacity, no ceiling tick (§2). The user learns the number from the left slot of the caption line already under the bar: "Fable 55%", zero extra height (§3). The option is a sub-switch "Inside the Weekly bar" under "Show model limits", per account, default off, Claude accounts only in v1 (§6, §9): ChatGPT's and Antigravity's caps are independent limits, not a share of the week, so they keep separate rows (§9). The tint goes on the app tile (Android, iOS, inner panes) and the iOS Bars, Strip and Overview widgets, which are the surfaces that draw a model row today; Summary, share card, lock screen and the notification are unchanged (§5). The Fable chart chip stays (§4).

2 · Tint anatomy

Leading, as Claude does. The solid part ends at the fill edge where the pace tick, the red over-pace slice, the dashed room and the Usage end mark all live; a trailing tint would sit under the red slice and the end mark. Leading leaves every existing mark where it is. Colour: the tint is the fill's own hue at 55% opacity over the card (drawn as the card colour at 45% over the fill's leading segment), so the bar carries three steps of one hue: track 25%, tint 55%, fill 100%. It is never a second colour, so the "no accent may equal a severity colour" rule (BEHAVIOR-SPEC §5) gains no new case. Ceiling tick: none (the share is unverified, and a fixed mark at 50% would mostly sit under the fill; the bar already carries the pace tick, the red slice and the end mark). Edges: the tint takes the fill's rounded left end and has a flat right edge. Minimum: a tint narrower than the bar's height is not drawn (a lighter dot at the start reads as a glitch); the caption still says "Fable 2%". Severity: at > 80 the fill turns yellow, > 90 orange, 100 red (BEHAVIOR-SPEC §5), and the tint follows, as the screenshot shows at 88%. The cap's own severity rides on the caption's number (§3), never on the tint.

4 · The trade-off, side by side — row on / combined on

What the combined mode gives up, drawn: the Fable row's own pace line (its V1 caption), its reset and projection (in the open tile, when the Fable chip is chosen), its press readout, and its dashed room. What it keeps: the Fable reading, the tint's width, and — recommended — the Fable chart chip ("7d · Fable · 5h"), so the open tile can still chart Fable with its pace and projection lines; the chip is not part of the row, and the one place Fable's pace survives costs nothing. Alternative: the chip goes with the row (then the chips read "7d · 5h" and the inner screen's second chart column goes too).

6 · The setting

Where: under the existing per-account "Show model limits" switch — Android in the account card's ⋮ → "Headline…" sheet, group MODEL LIMITS (app.md §6.2); iOS on the account page, section Model limits. Form: a sub-switch "Inside the Weekly bar", indented 20 dp / pt, shown only while "Show model limits" is on and the account is a Claude account with a cap. Default: off (the rows stay; nothing changes until chosen, as CCRM-99 did). Stored: per account, modelLimitsInline (bool, default false) beside showModelLimits; BEHAVIOR-MOBILE §5 "Model limits" gains the line. Robin wrote "applies to every Claude account": read here as available on every Claude account; if he means one value for all of them, the sub-switch moves to Appearance as "Model limits inside the Weekly bar" (global, Claude accounts only) and the per-account switch keeps on / off — decision D6.

Copy. On: "Fable's estimated share of the week shades the start of the Weekly bar, and its own row goes. Fable's pace, projection and reset are not shown." Off: "Fable keeps its own row and bar under Weekly." Two caps: "Fable's estimated share … ; Opus 4.5 keeps its row." The word estimated is the honesty of §1 in the UI.

7 · Narrow width and large text

Bar column widths (card padding 16, page margins 16): Android 360 dp → ring 56, gap 12 → 228 dp; 411 dp → 261; iPhone 17e 375 pt → 211 pt; iPhone SE 320 pt → 170 pt. The tint at 27.5% is 63 / 72 / 58 / 47 wide. Caption-line fit (estimates ± 6 dp, Roboto / SF at 12 sp / 12.5 pt): "Fable 55%" ≈ 56 dp / 58 pt; "57% pace mark · 5 under" ≈ 132 / 136; gap 8. Rule: both on one line when they fit, else the left slot takes a line of its own above the right one (a `FlowRow` / `ViewThatFits`, the pace-line fit idiom of §1.2). It wraps on a 360 dp phone from about 1.3× font scale, on the SE at the default size, on the 17e from about 1.1×. Even wrapped, the combined tile is shorter than the separate rows by ≈ 43 dp.

9 · Other accounts: can a combined bar honestly show their caps?

Answer: not as a nested tint. ChatGPT's "gpt-6-thinking" and Antigravity's "Argon" report their own percent of their own limit with their own reset (§1); nothing says what fraction of the Weekly budget that use is, or that it is inside the Weekly figure at all. A leading tint means "this much of the week was this model"; for these caps that is a claim the data cannot back. What can be drawn instead:

10 · Decisions for Robin

  1. D1 · The share. Capture your own usage payload before the build and check for a share or breakdown field. If none: accept the 50% as a Claude constant in core, flagged as an assumption with a fixture (option A), or choose B (a user-set share) or C (no share, inner track).
  2. D2 · Anatomy. Leading tint, the fill's hue at 55%, flat right edge, not drawn under the bar's height, clamped to the fill on skew, no ceiling tick. Yes / change.
  3. D3 · Legend. L1: "Fable 55%" in the caption line's left slot, the number in the cap's severity colour, no derived "≈27%" written anywhere; spoken form says the cap's percent only. Or L2 (the label) / L3 (press only).
  4. D4 · Several caps. S1: the first cap goes inside, the rest keep rows. Or S1c: two or more caps keep every row.
  5. D5 · The Fable chart chip stays under the open tile (Fable's pace and projection survive there), or goes with the row.
  6. D6 · The setting. A per-account sub-switch "Inside the Weekly bar" under "Show model limits", default off, Claude accounts with a cap only; copy as §6. Or one global value in Appearance for all Claude accounts; or the three-way choice.
  7. D7 · Surfaces. Tint on the app tile (both platforms, inner panes) and the iOS Bars, Strip and Overview widgets; everything else unchanged, the notification included. Yes / add the notification now.
  8. D8 · Other accounts. O3: Claude-only in v1; ChatGPT and Gemini keep rows. Or O1 everywhere as a second mode.
