CCRM-60 (Dual Identity) — proposed · revises CCRM-45 (Tracker Icon) and CCRM-56 (Provider Identity) decision 3

Cooldown, two ways — an icon and an app for Claude and ChatGPT

Google's Antigravity was dropped on 2026-09-08 under CCRM-55 (Antigravity Account), so the third colour leaves the identity and the blue band at the bottom of today's hourglass is now a lie. That leaves a cleaner brief, revised on 2026-09-09: two services, both named on the tile by their own marks, and the usage reading drawn into it, so the icon looks like a tracker issued for the job rather than a picture of one.

Not in this document. The always-on notification, including its option to show two accounts at once, is designed in design/settings-diet-wireframe.html section 2. This document is the icon and the app screens only.
What still constrains the icon. 1. No readable percentage at 16 px, and nothing that reads as progress: waived 2026-09-09 by decision, see section 2. 2. No provider mark on the launcher tile, the settled answer under CCRM-45 (Tracker Icon): waived 2026-09-09 by decision, see section 2. 3. Adaptive icon: 108 dp viewport, 66 dp safe circle, plus a themed monochrome layer where the launcher strips colour and keeps alpha. So "two" has to survive as an alpha difference, not only as a hue difference. 4. Whichever option wins, the About screen's drawable/ic_launcher.xml is redrawn to match it. 5. The status-bar glyph is the live usage gauge, not the logo. It is untouched by all of this (CCRM-49 (Glyph Legibility)).

2 · Icon, chosen horizontal split · half-marks at the seam · a thick inset capsule bar

Chosen 2026-09-09, round 7 variant 5. The tile is the Claude tile and the ChatGPT tile cut across the middle and stacked, with the usage bar laid along the line where they meet: Claude terracotta #D97757 on top carrying the top half of the sunburst, ChatGPT green #10A37F below carrying the bottom half of the blossom, and between them a thick inset capsule bar drawn to the numbers the app's own bars already use. One change came with the decision: the red segment now covers 35% of the bar, because a sliver of red is not what the tile is for. The tick sits at 45% and the fill ends at 80%.

The ground, drawn once. Horizontal split, seam at y 54: terracotta #D97757 over y 0 to 54, ChatGPT green #10A37F over y 54 to 108. The green is a deliberate departure from the store tile's near-black, which under terracotta reads as a shadow rather than as a second service. Each mark is placed so its full glyph would be 60 units tall, centred on x 54 with its own glyph centre on y 54, so the seam cuts it at its middle and 30 units of it exist on each side: the sunburst fills its 24-unit box and lands at translate(24,24) scale(2.5), the blossom is 16.5 units tall inside its box and centred in it and lands at translate(10.38,10.36) scale(3.6364), so both span y 24 to 84 and x 24 to 84. Each is clipped at the seam and drawn lightly, at 0.45, cream #FFF6F1 on the terracotta and white #FFFFFF on the green. The marks return to the tile by this decision, which waives the no-marks answer CCRM-45 (Tracker Icon) settled; they are the same two paths the app ships under CCRM-56 (Provider Identity), unmodified in shape.

The bar, drawn once. A capsule from x 10 to 98, 88 long, 16 units tall, y 46 to 62, centred on the seam, radius h/2 = 8 so both ends are fully round. Track translucent black at 28%. The cream #FFF6F1 fill runs from the left cap to the tick, so cream is visible from x 10 to 49.6; the fill's own capsule ends at x 80.4, which is 80% of the bar. The red #FF5252 runs from the tick at x 49.6, which is 45%, to x 80.4, so it is 30.8 units, 35% of the bar, and because it is clipped to the fill's capsule it takes the fill's round cap at its right end while the cream-to-red boundary stays a straight vertical edge. The ink #26211E tick at 70% is 4.96 units wide, x 47.12 to 52.08, and 25.6 units tall, y 41.2 to 66.8, so it overhangs 4.8 units past each long edge and straddles the boundary it marks. Draw order is track, fill, red, tick.

Where those numbers come from. BarGeometry.kt gives the tick as max(2, 0.31 × h) wide with 0.3 × h of overhang past each long edge, which on a 16-unit bar is 4.96 wide and 25.6 tall, 1.6 times the bar's own height. BarRenderer.kt draws it last, centred on the pace fraction, as a capsule of radius half its width, and it picks the colour from the theme, not the bar: #F2F2F4 at 60% when dark, #1D1D1F at 48% when light. A cream bar is a light surface and the tile has no theme to inherit, which is why the chosen tick is the light branch in the tile's own warm ink at a little more weight. The same file makes the track the fill's colour at 25% at radius h/2, the fill a capsule with a minimum visual width of h, and the red a plain rect from the pace line to the fill's edge clipped to the fill's capsule. Two departures on the tile, both deliberate: the track is black at 28% rather than cream at 25%, because here it crosses terracotta and green and a cream track washes both grounds out; and 16 of 108 units is a far chunkier bar than the app's 10 dp of about 370, so what is matched is the grammar inside the bar, the 0.31 and 0.3 ratios, the h/2 radii and the clipped red, not its aspect ratio. release/screenshots/app-tabs-personal.png and chart-above-pace-work-fold-inner.png predate CCRM-43 (Bar Pace Marks): they confirm the capsule track and the fill's round cap and have no tick in them at all, so every tick number here is the code's.

The meter rule. The tile is static, so the bar is a sample reading and never a live one: 80% used with 45% of the window elapsed, which is above pace, so the red segment and its round cap sit on the launcher tile permanently and neither the fill nor the tick ever moves. The old objection to a meter in the launcher tile was that the OS swapped the tile in for the status-bar glyph, which CCBG-12 (Status Icon Swap) fixed, so it no longer applies and CCRM-42 (App Icon) stays superseded. Adaptive icon: 108 unit viewport, 66 dp safe circle, 72 dp visible circle. The bar's caps are 44 units from the centre, so a launcher that masks to a circle cuts the bar at x 18.9 and 89.1 and takes both round ends with it; the reading itself, the tick at 49.6 and the red's cap at 80.4, stays inside.

Why this one. The bar is thick enough to be a band rather than a line, 7.1 px at 48 dp, and inset so the seam behind it is a plain colour meeting. The ink tick is the one thing the four other round-7 variants could not fix: a translucent white tick sitting half on a cream fill has almost nothing to be seen against, and ink at 70% reads on the cream, on the red and on both grounds where it overhangs.

Geometry. Bar x 10 to 98, 16 tall, y 46 to 62, radius 8, track black 28%. Cream visible x 10 to 49.6; fill capsule ends x 80.4; red x 49.6 to 80.4, 30.8 units, with the fill's round cap; ink tick 70%, x 47.12 to 52.08, y 41.2 to 66.8. Marks at 0.45, each clipped at y 54.

Known cost: a round mask cuts both caps, and the tick is now the darkest thing on the tile, so it competes with the red for the eye's first stop. Both were accepted on review.

Monochrome rule: one colour and an alpha, so top band 0.55, bottom band 0.3, marks 0.2, track 0.32, cream fill solid, red segment 0.85, tick solid. The tick goes from 70% ink to solid because a partial ink tick has nothing to be partial against once the layer has one colour, and the 1.0 to 0.85 step under the red is small, so the tick's overhangs above and below the bar do the real separating.

What this means in code

  • Adaptive icon. Background layer is the two grounds; foreground is the two clipped half-marks and the bar; the monochrome layer follows the rule above. drawable/ic_launcher.xml, which the About screen draws, is redrawn to match, per constraint 4 at the top of this document.
  • Not the status bar. The status-bar glyph stays the live usage gauge under CCRM-49 (Glyph Legibility). Nothing here touches it.

3 · The app, two rooms approved 2026-09-09 · same cards, two atmospheres

The shell already themes from the selected tab's account under CCRM-56 (Provider Identity) decision 1, so a ChatGPT tab already turns the bars and the chart green. Approved 2026-09-09: the app takes that one step further, so that a Claude tab and a ChatGPT tab feel like two rooms in one house, serif headline figures on the Claude room included. Nothing about what a card contains changes. Same windows, same pace tick, same reset rows, same trend block, same rules about which cards exist. Nothing on any card is removed or simplified: the chart, its guides, its axis and its projection line are exactly today's.

  • Claude room. Surface tint warm ivory, #F5EFE8 light and #1B1715 dark, card surfaces one step warmer than the surface. Headline percentages in the system serif, Android FontFamily.Serif, which is Noto Serif on the device and is mocked here with Georgia. Bar fill and tab indicator Claude orange.
  • ChatGPT room. Surface tint neutral, #F7F7F8 light and #0D0D0D dark. Headline percentages in the default sans. Bar fill and tab indicator ChatGPT green.
  • Identical in both. The cards, the pace tick in neutral ink, the reset rows, the trend block, the severity reds. Provider marks keep their own colour in both rooms: marks identify, colour themes.
  • One thing this changes in shipped code. Today the tab strip's text and indicator are pinned to neutral onSurface on purpose, so hues do not crossfade mid-swipe. The mocks below give the indicator the room accent and leave the labels neutral, which is the narrowest version of that change. If the crossfade is still unacceptable, the indicator stays neutral and the rooms are carried by surface, serif and bars alone.

State 6 · narrow check, stated not drawn

At 410 dp the three tabs above get about 136 dp each and a label of up to sixteen characters needs about 110 dp, so "Personal", "Work" and "ChatGPT" fit in a fixed strip with no truncation.
