Now on iPhone and iPad

Cooldown 2.0 runs on iPhone and iPad as a public beta on TestFlight. It has the Summary, home-screen and lock-screen widgets, a Live Activity and a Control Center control.

The Summary with five accounts
New in 2.0

Every account on one page

With two or more accounts, Cooldown opens on the Summary. It lists every account's week on one page. Sort by most room, and the account to use next comes first.

New in 2.0

Time ahead or behind

Cooldown compares your weekly use with an even pace. The gap shows as time, which says more than a percentage.

1d 12h behindYou have room to spare
14h 48m aheadSlow down
New in 2.0

The ring and the bar

The ring is the 5-hour limit, where the plan has one. The bar is the week, and a tick marks an even pace. The same two shapes appear in the app, the notification and the widgets.

Widgets for your home screen

On Android, a widget shows one account or all of them, as a ring, a number, a countdown or a row of rings. It fills the size you give it.

Android shown. iPhone and iPad have their own home-screen and lock-screen widgets.
Redrawn in 2.0

Two accounts in one notification

The always-on notification can show two accounts at once. Each has its own weekly bar and big figure. Expand it to see time ahead or behind, both reset times and a Refresh button.

Android only.
Collapsed, then expanded
New in 2.0

The Pace map

The Pace map plots each account as a dot against its own week. Switch on figures, trails or a projection to see more.

It sits under the Summary.
What it reads

Cooldown only reads. It sends no prompts and uses no quota. It has no server. Your sign-ins stay on your device, encrypted, and go only to the service they belong to.

New to Cooldown?

Install the app, then sign in to each account on that service's own page. Setup takes about two minutes.

To look around first, tap Try with sample data. It shows three made-up accounts and sends nothing.
