Cooldown
Beta · Android, iPhone and iPad

Know where your
week stands.

Cooldown shows the weekly and 5-hour limits of your own Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini plans in one place. It tells you when each one resets and whether you are using it faster or slower than an even pace.

Unofficial integration. Not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Free and open source

What you get

Sign in once per account. Cooldown keeps the numbers fresh in the app, a notification and your home screen.

The Summary

One page lists every account you track. The account with the most room to spare comes first, so you know which one to use next.

Time ahead or behind

Cooldown turns your weekly use into time against an even pace: 1d 12h behind means you have room, 14h 48m ahead means slow down. A percentage alone cannot tell you that.

The Pace map

A chart places each account against an even pace through its week. Tap a dot to open that account.

Ring and bar

The ring is the 5-hour limit, where the plan has one. The bar is the week, with a tick that marks an even pace.

Two accounts at once

On Android, an always-on notification shows two accounts side by side. The status bar shows the 5-hour ring, or the week on a plan without one.

Widgets

Rings, bars and reset times sit on your home screen. Pick one account or several, in sizes from small to wide.

Room to spread out

On a foldable or a tablet, an account's limits and their charts sit side by side. Light and dark themes follow your phone.

What it reads

Cooldown only reads. It sends no prompts and uses no quota. It has no server. Your sign-ins stay on your device, encrypted, and go only to the service they belong to.

What it needs

Android 12 or later, or iOS and iPadOS 18 or later. A Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini subscription.

Get the beta

Scan the code or go to github.com/robineam360/Cooldown. Android: download the APK from Releases. iPhone and iPad: join the TestFlight beta, listed as EAM360 Cooldown.

We plan to release on Google Play and the App Store once Anthropic, OpenAI and Google have each reviewed and approved how Cooldown reads plan usage from their services.

No provider has approved this way of reading usage yet. A provider can change or close it at any time, or act on an account that uses it. Read "Before you install" on the GitHub page first.
