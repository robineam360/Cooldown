What Cooldown does

Cooldown shows how much of your Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini plan limits you have used, and when they reset. It reads your own accounts and puts the weekly and 5-hour limits on one screen. It runs on Android, iPhone and iPad, and it is free and open source.

Before you install

None of the three services offers a public way for an app to read your plan usage. Cooldown signs in the way each service's own command-line tool does, on that service's own page, and reads the usage numbers its apps show. For Gemini it must also send that tool's identifier, so this sign-in is the least certain of the three. No provider has approved this. A provider can change or close this route at any time, sign Cooldown out, or act on an account that uses it. Use it at your own discretion.

Cooldown only reads. It sends no prompts and uses no quota. It has no server. Your sign-ins stay on your device, encrypted, and go only to the service they belong to.

Sign in

Open Settings, then Accounts, then Add account. Pick the service. Each account gets its own tab, its own part of the notification and its own reset alerts.

Claude

  1. Pick Claude. If you have several browsers, choose the one where that account is signed in.
  2. On Claude's page, tap Authorize. Cooldown asks for profile access only.
  3. Copy the code, return to the app, paste it and tap Finish sign-in.

Pro, Max and Team plans work. A Free plan signs in, but Claude returns no usage for it.

ChatGPT

  1. Once, turn on device code sign-in in ChatGPT, under Settings and Security. A work account needs its admin to allow it.
  2. Pick ChatGPT. The app shows a code that stays valid for 15 minutes.
  3. Open auth.openai.com/codex/device on this phone or any device. Enter the code and approve.

Gemini

  1. Pick Gemini. Google's own page opens in your browser.
  2. Sign in, approve, copy the code it shows, then paste it in the app. Cooldown never sees your password.

The card shows the plan, a status dot and the last check. Re-sign in uses the same steps as the first sign-in. Remove an account from its menu.

An account's screen

The big bar is the weekly limit. The ring is the 5-hour limit. The tick on the bar marks an even pace. The chart plots every reading against the even-pace line.

The 5-hour ring shows where the plan has one. OpenAI suspended the 5-hour limit for many plans.

Cooldown · User guide · Version 2.0 beta6

Time ahead or behind

A percentage tells you how much is gone. Cooldown also tells you how that compares with an even pace, as time.

"1d 12h behind · resets Sun 9:30 PM" means you have used less than an even pace would have by now. "14h 48m ahead" means you have used more. If you paused, an even pace would catch up in 14 hours 48 minutes.

Behind means less used than an even pace, and it shows in green. Ahead means more used, and it shows in red.

1d 12h behind · resets Sun 9:30 PM
On the mark · resets Sat 8:00 AM
14h 48m ahead · resets Wed 6:30 PM

This line applies to weekly windows. The 5-hour ring keeps its own projection.

Under each bar, "65% pace mark · 56 under" gives the mark's place and how far under it you are. Under Appearance, Usage display, Vs pace shows the big figure as your distance from the mark.

The Summary and Pace map

The Summary

With two or more accounts the app opens on All. Every account's week sits on one page, ranked by the most room for the time left. A sort menu also offers your own order, resets soonest and most used.

The Pace map

Each account is a dot plotted against its own week. A dot under the even-pace line is behind. A dot above it is ahead. Figures, Trails and Projection switch its layers. Tap a dot for a callout, and tap again to open the account.

An account at 0% this week leaves the map. The line under the map counts them.

Tags

Tag an account Personal or Work. Then filter the Summary by either tag.

Share snapshot

Share snapshot sends the whole Summary as one image. Account names are shown. Email addresses are never shown.

Cooldown · User guide · Version 2.0 beta8

The always-on notification

In the shade

Turn it on under Settings, Alerts. Pick a first account and, if you like, a second. The weekly window is the bar and the big figure. The 5-hour window is a ring on the left. Expanded, each account adds its pace line and reset time, and one Refresh button.

Status-bar icon

The icon shows the 5-hour ring, or the weekly bar for a plan with no 5-hour window. It can follow the first account, the second, or whichever is higher.

Reset alerts

Each account and window can ping when it resets: Off, If busy or Always. If busy pings only when the window had reached 80%.

Tapping a number

Each half opens Cooldown on that account's tab. You can choose to open that service's own app instead.

Cooldown · User guide · Version 2.0 beta9

Widgets

On Android

Long-press the home screen, open Widgets and pick Cooldown. Drop a widget at any size, from 1×1 to a full row. Its settings open at once. Choose the account, the window and the background.

The four faces

  • Ring: one account as a gauge.
  • Number: the big percentage.
  • Countdown: when the window comes back, ticking live.
  • All accounts: one ring per account, side by side.

The 5-hour window is a ring and the week is a bar, as in the notification.

Keeping them fresh

If you use widgets without the always-on notification, add Cooldown to the never sleeping apps in Android's battery settings. Otherwise the system can pause the background refresh.

On iPhone and iPad

Cooldown offers home-screen and lock-screen widgets, a Live Activity and a Control Center control.

Settings and fixes

Accounts holds sign-in, plan, tag and order.

Alerts holds the notification, the status-bar icon and reset alerts.

Appearance holds theme, density, chart options, time format and Usage display.

More holds refresh rate, updates, About and the privacy policy.

If something looks wrong

A half looks dimmedRefreshes failed for six hours. You see the last known numbers. It fixes itself on the next good refresh.
Re-auth neededThe sign-in expired. Tap Re-sign in on that account's card.
Free plan messageClaude returns no usage for a Free plan. Move to Pro, Max or Team, or remove the account.
Rate limitedThe service asked the app to wait, and the app retries. If renewing the sign-in keeps failing, tap Re-sign in.
A widget stopped updatingAdd Cooldown to the never sleeping apps in Android's battery settings.
Cannot open saved sign-insAndroid's secure storage is not answering. Your sign-ins are still there. Tap Try again to check now.
No reset alert arrivedCheck notification permission and that the reset is not Off. If busy skips windows that never reached 80%.
A crash card appearsTap Share report to send it to the developer. It holds no sign-in and no email.

Anything else: Settings, More, Diagnostics shares the app log.

Privacy and notices

What it reads

Cooldown only reads. It sends no prompts and uses no quota. It has no server. Your sign-ins stay on your device, encrypted, and go only to the service they belong to.

There is no analytics and no tracking. A crash report stays on your device until you share it. Remove account deletes that account's data from the device.
