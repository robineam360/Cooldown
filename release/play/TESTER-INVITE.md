## 1. The ask (send now, before the build is ready)

```text
Subject: Help me get Cooldown onto Google Play? (2 minutes, then 14 days of just keeping it)

Hi <name>,

I'm putting Cooldown — my little app that shows your Claude / ChatGPT / Gemini usage limits and
when they reset — on Google Play. Google makes new developers run a closed test first: at least
12 people have to stay signed up as testers for 14 days in a row.

Would you be one of them? All it takes:
• an Android phone (Android 12 or newer)
• the Google account you use for the Play Store on that phone — reply with its email address
• staying in the test for 14 days. You don't have to use it every day; just don't leave the test
  or uninstall it in those two weeks.

It's free, there are no ads, and your sign-in stays on your phone. It's most useful if you pay for
Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

I'll send the link in a couple of weeks when the new version (2.0) is ready.

Thanks!
Robin
```

   and have a look at the Summary tab.

Then just keep it installed and stay in the test until <Day S + 14, a date>. That's the bit that
counts for Google.

Anything odd, confusing, or broken? Reply to this email — or use "Send feedback" on the app's

Thanks for helping,
Robin
