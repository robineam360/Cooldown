**Beta App Description** (4,000 characters at most; this is 1,085):

Cooldown shows how much of your Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini usage windows you have used, and when each one resets, on your iPhone, iPad, Home Screen, Lock Screen and Live Activity. It is a read-only monitor: it never sends a prompt, never spends quota, and has no server of its own. Nothing about your usage is sent to the developer.

What you get: each account's Weekly bar and 5-hour ring with a pace mark that tells you whether you are ahead of or behind an even spend; a trend chart; an All tab that ranks your accounts by room left; reset alerts; widgets (Number, Bars, Strip, Overview); a lock-screen countdown; a Control Center control; and a share card.

You sign in to your own accounts on each service's own page; the app never sees a password. Sign-ins stay in this device's Keychain and are sent only to the service they belong to. To look around without an account, tap "Try with sample data" on the first screen.

Cooldown is unofficial and is not affiliated with Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. This beta is for colleagues at EAM360; please do not share the invitation link.
