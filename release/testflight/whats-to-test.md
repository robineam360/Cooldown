# What to Test — Cooldown 2.0 (iPhone and iPad)

Cooldown shows how much of your Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini usage windows you have used, and when
they reset. It reads usage only: it never sends a prompt and never spends quota.

**Please try**
1. **Look around first:** on the first screen tap *Try with sample data* for three made-up accounts
   (nothing is saved). Exit puts you back.
2. **Sign in** (Settings → Add account) to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. Claude asks you to paste a code
   back; ChatGPT shows a short code to approve on a device you already use; Gemini opens Google's
   sign-in. Tell me if any step is confusing. Tap an account in Settings for its page: rename it, check
   now, sign in again.
3. **The main screen:** each account's Weekly bar and 5h ring, the pace mark, the trend chart (tap
   it), the credits card, and the All summary tab when you have two or more accounts.
4. **History:** the Plan Fit reading on the 7-day pane; on iPad, the 5-hour and 7-day columns side by side.
5. **Widgets:** add a Number, Bars, Strip or Overview widget to the Home Screen, and a lock-screen
   widget. They update when iOS lets them, so a few minutes' delay is normal.
6. **Reset alerts** (Settings → Reset alerts): leave one on and see whether it arrives when a window
   resets.
7. **Live Activity** (Settings → Lock Screen), the **Control Center** control, and the **share card**.
8. **iPad:** the sidebar, rotation, and Split View.

**Known gaps in this build**
- Widgets may show a stale reading until the app has been opened; iOS decides when they refresh.
- The Countdown widget still counts down the 5h window, not the Weekly one.
