REVIEW: full · plan=v20-feedback-wireframes

Scope and threat model: a design review of a mobile wireframe (Android + iOS/iPadOS usage-tracker app) before any code is built. Nothing here changes machine, money or data state; the risk is shipping a layout that fails at a state, a width or a font size nobody drew, or one that contradicts the owner's settled decisions or the shared core spec (which a desktop client also pins). Find: missing or broken states, widths that cannot hold what is drawn, contradictions between items, and spec changes the wireframe implies but does not name.

## How the wireframe draws them
Item 1. Height 52 dp unchanged. Row: ring 36 · 6 · ring 36 · 14 · rows (name line 15 dp, gap 4, bar 8) · 8 · 42 dp figure slot; chrome 136 dp, so the bar is width − 136: 192 dp on a 328 dp phone column, 216 on the foldable's inner shade (~352, derived, not measured), 136 at an assumed 272 dp cover (from an older pass, unverified), 114 at a pessimistic 250; floor 176 dp keeps a minimum bar. State words in precedence: Sign in · No reading · Stale · Out · Thu · Held · 3:40 PM · Not started. At the narrowest drawn width the name ellipsizes to one letter before "Held · 3:40 PM" drops its tail to "Held"; a second drawing shows the tails dropped first. A plan with no 5h keeps its ring slot with a track-and-cross ring. Dark shade drawn. Expanded: figure 24 sp gives a 186 dp text column (159 today + arithmetic, unmeasured); today's widest pace line (169 dp) keeps its weekday, but the longer since/until lines (190–196 dp) still drop it; closing two 14 dp gaps to 8 dp would hold them (not drawn). Largest font: the fixed 42 dp slot and 52 dp height cannot follow the font scale, so collapsed text is capped in dp — a departure from the foundations rule that text follows the user's size.

Item 2. "All · Fable · 5h" → "7d · Fable · 5h" on Android and iOS; one-chip, no-5h and limits-hidden states drawn; the stored selection key is unchanged; screen readers say "7-day, all models". The tile's lead label "Weekly · All models" is drawn unchanged (owner to confirm).

Item 3. Vertical lines at window start + k × 24 h (window-relative, not midnights), horizontal at k/7; labels: start date left, ordinals 2–6 centred in their columns, reset date right; the column holding "now" is bold; an ordinal within 16 dp of "now" or touching an end label gives way. Grid ink 13%, under the dashed 80/90/100 guides. 5h: five hourly columns with start and reset times. Small charts (72 dp) get the grid without ordinals. The Pace map gets the same sevenths both ways; its centre note "each account's own week" moves from the axis to the foot line, which changes the core spec's map wording.

Item 4 — premise conflict. The decision record says the line "already shows markTime". In code the clock after the dot is the window's reset (the duration comes from markTime). The wireframe draws A (as decided: the clock becomes the mark instant, "since 8:15 AM"/"until 2:57 PM"; the reset leaves the line; "On the mark · resets Sat 8:00 AM"; held/spent/signed-out/no-reading/not-started lines listed) and B ("· resets Sun 9:30 PM", the reset kept and named), with C (A plus a "resets" tail where it fits). A fit table gives the printed form for every surface (notification collapsed 142 dp, expanded 141, two-account expanded 159/186, widget 132, Summary word slot 90, snapshot 150, Summary line 3 at 279/228, iOS rows) at font scales 1.0/1.3/2.0 using the app's own width estimator (which ran 2% narrow on the device). Under A the clause never fits the 142 dp collapsed column for durations of an hour or more, so the collapsed notification shows only "5h 45m behind". The owner chooses A, B or C before build.

Item 5. Accounts under 1% weekly leave the map; the foot line right says "2 under 1%" (joined with "· 1 no reading" when both). All under 1%: the frame, grid and diagonal stay and a message sits in the behind wedge ("Nothing to plot yet / every account is under 1% this week"). One account: one dot. Spent: × on the dot. Signed out / no reading: off the map, as today.

Item 6. Tag None/Personal/Work via the account menu (Android "Tag…" dialog; iOS account page row and long-press submenu); neutral outline chip after the plan chip in Settings only, not on Summary rows. Filter: segmented 28 dp high beside "Weekly", shown once any account is tagged, remembered, filters list, map and snapshot; empty segment disabled at 38%; a remembered filter that loses its last account falls back to All. Sort: "your order ▾" menu with the four orders (writes the same setting Appearance keeps). Widths: 292 dp of header; on the 288 dp phone column the sort shows its glyph only ("⇅ ▾"); at large text the filter wraps under the title.

Item 7. Chips right on the map title line replacing the subtitle: Android 32 dp, 12 sp, 10 dp padding, 6 dp gaps (≈198 dp, fits 272 dp beside the title at scale 1.0), selected = tonal fill, no check glyph; iOS 13 pt capsules; wrap under the title when the line cannot hold them; remembered. Figures "Teams 52%" / "48% left" / "−13%" 11.5 sp bold in the account colour (severity colour in Used/Left; green/red/ink in vs pace). Trails as today, last 24 h heavier. Projection: dashed tail from the dot to a hollow ring at the projected % on the right edge, or to a red ring on the 100% line where it runs out; none when the estimate is null (< 20 min of samples or < 1 point of movement), spent or unlocked. Callout: tap dot or name (48 dp target, nearest wins); others dim to 35%; card with name + figure, pace line, "~74% at reset" or "100% at Wed 6:10 PM" in red; opens above-right, flips left at the right edge and below at the top, clamped to the plot; second tap opens the account; empty tap clears; screen-reader text given. Leaders: after right/below/left/above-left fail, the name tries 24, 36, 48 dp in eight directions; dotted, ≤ 48 dp, refused if it crosses a placed name or dot; with Projection on, a name does not sit on its own tail or ring; if nothing is free, the right-hand spot stands. Ten-account and 360 dp phone states drawn; iPad square map 340 × 340.

Item 8. Android: a top-bar ⋮ on the Summary tab with "Share snapshot" (hidden when no shown account has a reading), the same dialog as the per-account share card; iOS: the ellipsis menu's "Share card"; no reading → "Nothing to share yet". Card 360 dp at 4× (iOS 300 pt at 3×): header "All accounts" (or the filter's name), "as of 2:00 PM, Tue Nov 10", the order's name; rows in screen order and filter (ring 32, name 13 sp, figure 17 sp, bar 8 with tick, pace span left, "Resets …" right), one-line rows from six accounts; then the map 200 dp with the on-screen layers, never the chips, a pinned callout or its dimming; footer "COOLDOWN". Vs pace renders as Used, as the per-account card does. Spent, signed-out, no-reading, stale and synthetic states drawn; names shown, e-mail addresses never.
